**Jñāna-Santatiṃ**
Meaning: "the flow of knowledge"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 10)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: ज्ञान
Transliteration: Jñāna
Meaning: "knowing, becoming acquainted wth, knowledge, (esp.) the higher knowledge (derived from meditation on the one Universal Spirit)"
Transliteration: Saṃ-tati 
Meaning: "stretching or extending along, expanse, continuity, uninterruptedness"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: ज्ञानसन्ततिं 
Transliteration: Jñāna-Santatiṃ
Meaning: "the flow of knowledge or thought"
