Vāyu is the second gross element, evolving from ether and corresponding to the subtle element of touch (sparśa), experienced through the skin. It represents the principle of motion, dynamism, pressure, and gaseous expansion in the universe. In the macrocosm, vāyu manifests as winds, atmospheric currents, and the vital breath of nature that drives change and interaction. In the human body (microcosm), it governs all physiological movements—ranging from the gross movements of limbs and the beating of the heart to subtle internal flows like respiration, nerve impulses, and cellular activity (known collectively as the vāyus or prāṇas). Philosophically, vāyu symbolizes constant change, restlessness, and the dynamic life-force. Imbalances of air in Ayurvedic terms lead to mental anxiety and instability, making grounding practices essential for physical and spiritual harmony.
