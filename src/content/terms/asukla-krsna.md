# Aśukla-Kṛṣṇā - अशुक्लाकृष्ण (Neither White nor Black / Transcendent Karma)

Aśukla-kṛṣṇā karma refers to the unique actions of an advanced yogi or an enlightened being who is established in deep meditation and supreme integration (samādhi). As stated in Yoga Sūtra 4.7, "The action of a yogi is neither white nor dark, while the actions of others are of three kinds." Because the yogi acts without personal ego, without identification with the doer (ahaṅkāra), and completely renounces all desire for the fruits of action (niṣkāma karma), their deeds do not generate binding karmic seeds or latent impressions (anāśaya). Unlike ordinary actions driven by attachment (rāga) or aversion (dveṣa), the yogi's actions flow spontaneously from pure consciousness and universal compassion. They neither accumulate merit nor demerit, and thus they do not produce future binding reactions or rebirth. This non-binding, transcendental action breaks the chain of karma entirely, paving the way for absolute spiritual freedom (kaivalya).
