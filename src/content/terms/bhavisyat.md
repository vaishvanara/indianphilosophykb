**Bhaviṣyat** 
Meaning: "the future"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 1)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: भविष्यत् 
Transliteration: Bhaviṣyat 
Meaning: "about to become or be, future"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: भविष्यत् 
Transliteration: Bhaviṣyat 
Meaning: "the future"

**Translation by Swami Krishnananda**
Transliteration: Bhaviṣyat 
Meaning: "all that will be in the future"
