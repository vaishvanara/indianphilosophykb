**Yat** 
Meaning: "what"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 1)

Reference:
Sharvananda, Swami (1920). Mundaka and Mandukya Upanishads: With Sanskrit Text; Paraphrase with Word-For-Word Literal Translation, English, Rendering and Comments. Mylapore, Madras: Sri Ramakrishna Math

---------------------

According to the search results, the Sanskrit word यत् (yat) has several meanings:
- To attempt, endeavor, strive, try (usually with infinitive or dative) 
- To strive after, be eager or anxious for, long for 
- To exert oneself, persevere, labor 
- To observe caution, be watchful 
- To go, proceed 
- The root यत् (yat) is likely related to the root यम् (yam), meaning "to stretch" .

Some other meanings and usages of यत् (yat):
- As a conjunction, meaning "because, as, since, wherefore" 
- As an interjection expressing pleasure, alarm or restlessness 
- To place in order, marshal, join, connect 
- To keep pace, be in line, rival or vie with 
- To conform or comply with 
- To meet, encounter (in battle)
