**Anuttara** 
Translation: ““beyond which there is nothing”; the Highest, the supreme, the Absolute”
Definition: A name for Reality, according to Kashmir Śaivism, beyond which there is nothing.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
