**Āgāmi Karma** 
Meaning: "karma or actions being performed in the present life, which will bear fruit in the future"

It is one of the three types of karma in Hindu philosophy, along with Sañcita karma (accumulated past karma) and Prārabdha karma (karma that has begun to manifest in the present life). The key points about Āgāmi karma are:

- Āgāmi karma represents the new actions and intentions being generated in the current lifetime.
- It is the karma that is being created in the present through one's thoughts, words and deeds. This karma is yet to bear fruit.
- Unlike Prārabdha karma which must be experienced, Āgāmi karma can be modified or eliminated through spiritual practices and conscious effort.
- By cultivating positive Āgāmi karma through virtuous actions, one can counteract the effects of past negative Sañcita karma.
- Āgāmi karma is considered the only type of karma that is fully under one's control in the present moment. It determines future births and experiences.
- Eliminating Āgāmi karma, along with exhausting Prārabdha and Sañcita karma, is necessary to attain liberation (moksha) from the cycle of rebirth.

So in summary, Āgāmi karma refers to the new karmic imprints being created in the present life, which will yield results in the future, unless transformed through spiritual practice and self-effort.
