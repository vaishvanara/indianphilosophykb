**Brahma-Jñāna**
Meaning: “knowledge of the Absolute; Self-realization”
 
- ब्रह्म – Brahma – the ultimate Reality; the ground of the universe; the Absolute; the Divine; “great” (from the root bṛh = “to expand, greater than the greatest”)
- ज्ञान – Jñāna – knowledge; wisdom; comprehension (from the verb root jñā – “to know”)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
