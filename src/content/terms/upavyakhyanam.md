**Upavyākhyānaṃ**
Meaning: "explanation"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 1)

Reference:
Sharvananda, Swami (1920). Mundaka and Mandukya Upanishads: With Sanskrit Text; Paraphrase with Word-For-Word Literal Translation, English, Rendering and Comments. Mylapore, Madras: Sri Ramakrishna Math

---------------------
According to the search results, उपव्याख्यानं (upavyākhyānaṃ) means "a supplementary explanation" or "a commentary". Some key points:

It is a Sanskrit word derived from the root √khyā, meaning "to explain".

In the Chāndogya Upaniṣad, उपव्याख्यानं is used to refer to the Chāndogya Upaniṣad itself as a commentary or explanation of the sacred syllable ओम् (Oṃ).

The Chāndogya Upaniṣad states that "this (Chāndogya Upaniṣad) is an upavyākhyāna (commentary) of that (Oṃ)".

In other contexts, उपव्याख्यानं simply means "commentary" without any specific reference.

So in summary, उपव्याख्यानं denotes a supplementary explanation, interpretation or commentary, especially in the context of sacred Hindu texts like the Upaniṣads. The Chāndogya Upaniṣad itself is referred to as an उपव्याख्यानं of the mystical syllable ओम्.
