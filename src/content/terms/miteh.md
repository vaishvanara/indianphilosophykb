**Miteḥ**
Meaning: "from its being a measure"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 11)

Reference:
Sharvananda, Swami (1920). Mundaka and Mandukya Upanishads: With Sanskrit Text; Paraphrase with Word-For-Word Literal Translation, English, Rendering and Comments. Mylapore, Madras: Sri Ramakrishna Math

---------------------
मितेः (miteḥ) is a genitive singular form of the Sanskrit word मिति (miti), which means "friend" or "companion".

In various contexts, मितेः can be translated to:

- Of a friend
- Belonging to a friend
- Relating to a friend
- Concerning a friend
- In relation to a friend

For example, in the Mahabharata (12.250.13), it is written:

मितेः प्रजानां च

Miteḥ prajānāṃ ca

Which translates to: "And of the friends of the people."

In general, मितेः refers to a person's relationship with a friend or companion, and is often used to describe a bond of friendship or camaraderie.
