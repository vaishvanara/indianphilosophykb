**Mahāpralaya**
Meaning: “final cosmic dissolution”
Definition: The end of a world age or Manvantara. The end of a cosmic age or Kalpa. It is usually used to designate the dissolution of a cosmic age. The great flood. It is also the great destruction of the universe when God reabsorbs all names and forms into His being. 

- महा – Mahā – “great”; mighty; powerful; lofty; noble
- प्रलय – Pralaya – periodic cosmic dissolution (from the verb root lī = “to dissolve” + pra = “away”)
