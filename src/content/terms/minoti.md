Meaning: "measures (by his knowledge)" or "he measures;" in the *Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad*, describing one who realizes *Prājña* as the third letter ('M') of AUM.

See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 11)](mandukyopanisat.md)

---

## Definition

*Minoti* (मिनोति) is a Sanskrit finite verb meaning "he measures," "he metes out," "he bounds," "he estimates," or "he comprehends." 

Lexically, the verb denotes the act of taking the measure of an object, establishing its boundaries, or coming to a full cognitive grasp of its nature.

Contextually within the *Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad* (Mantra 11), *minoti* occurs in the statement of spiritual fruit (*phala-śruti*) granted to one who meditates on the identity between *Prājña* (consciousness in deep sleep, *suṣuptasthāna*) and *makāra* (the letter *m*, the third *mātrā* of *AUM*):

> *suṣuptasthāno prājño makāro tṛtīyā mātrā miter apīter vā minoti ha vā idaṁ sarvam apītiśca bhavati ya evaṁ veda* (Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad, 11)

In this passage, *minoti* ("he measures" or "comprehends") expresses the first of two grounds of comparison (*sāmānya*) between *makāra* and *Prājña*: *miti* (measurement). Śaṅkarācārya explains in his *Māṇḍūkyopaniṣad-bhāṣya* that just as a standard measure (such as a *prastha*) measures grain, *Prājña* (deep sleep) serves as the measure or bounding container of the waking (*Viśva*) and dream (*Taijasa*) states during their evolution (*sṛṣṭi*) and involution (*laya*). Similarly, when reciting *AUM*, the sound *m* (*makāra*) bounds and completes the preceding letters *a* (*akāra*) and *u* (*ukāra*). Thus, one who realizes this non-dual truth "measures all this" (*minoti ha vā idaṁ sarvam*)—that is, comprehends the true nature and origin of the manifested universe.

## Etymology

Grammatically, *minoti* (मिनोति) is a 3rd person singular present indicative Parasmaipada verb (*laṭ, prathama-puruṣa ekavacana*) derived from:

1. **Root**: *Mi* (मि, Class 5 Parasmaipada) or *Mā* (मा, "to measure, mete out, mark off, or perceive").
2. **Conjugational Class**: As a verb of the fifth conjugational class (*svādi-gaṇa*), the root takes the class vikaraṇa affix *-nu-* (णु), which undergoes guṇa strengthening to *-no-* before strong personal terminations in the present tense (*mi* + *no*).
3. **Personal Termination**: The primary 3rd person singular active ending *-ti* (ति) is attached to the stem (*mi-no-ti* = *minoti*, "he measures").

Lexicographical sources (such as Macdonell's *Sanskrit-English Dictionary*) record *minoti* under the root √*mi* (1. *MI*, "fix, erect, measure, perceive") as well as in relation to √*mā* ("measure, limit, infer"). Related nominal formations from the same verbal root include *miti* (feminine noun, "measurement, dimension, cognition"), *mita* (past passive participle, "measured, bounded"), and *mātrā* (feminine noun, "measure, unit, letter").
