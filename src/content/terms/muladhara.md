**Mūlādhāra**
Meaning: “the center located at the base of the spine; the “seat of the root”; the seat of the earth; (mūla = root or source and ādhāra = to support)”
Definition: The spiritual center at the base of the spine where the Kuṇḍalinī lies dormant.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
