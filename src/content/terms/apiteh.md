**Apīteḥ**
Meaning: "on account of its being the final"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 11)

Reference:
Sharvananda, Swami (1920). Mundaka and Mandukya Upanishads: With Sanskrit Text; Paraphrase with Word-For-Word Literal Translation, English, Rendering and Comments. Mylapore, Madras: Sri Ramakrishna Math

----------------------
अपीतेः (apīteḥ) is a genitive singular form of the Sanskrit word अपीति (apīti), which means "not a friend" or "not a companion".

In various contexts, अपीतेः can be translated to:

- Of one who is not a friend
- Belonging to one who is not a friend
- Relating to one who is not a friend
- Concerning one who is not a friend
- In relation to one who is not a friend

For example, in the Bhagavad Gita (6.32), it is written:

अपीतेः प्रजानां च

Apīteḥ prajānāṃ ca

Which translates to: "And of those who are not friends of the people."

In general, अपीतेः refers to a person's relationship with someone who is not a friend or companion, and is often used to describe a lack of friendship or camaraderie.
