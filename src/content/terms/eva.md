**Eva**
Meaning: "thus"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verses 1, 5, 9, 10, 11, 12)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: एव
Transliteration: Eva 
Meaning: "so, just so, exactly so (in the sense of the later evam)"
Sanskrit: एवं 
Transliteration: Evaṃ
Meaning: "thus, in this way, in such a manner, such"

**Definition from A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy (John Grimes)**
Sanskrit: एव
Transliteration: Eva 
Meaning: "only; in fact; without limitation"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: एव
Transliteration: Eva 
Meaning: "verily, alone, thus"
