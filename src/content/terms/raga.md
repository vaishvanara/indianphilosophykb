**Rāga**
Meaning: "attachment"

1. **In music**, रागः refers to a melodic framework with fixed notes, characteristic musical phrases, and mood. It is a fundamental concept in Indian classical music and serves as the basis for improvisation and composition
2. **In Vedanta philosophy**, रागः means "passion", "attachment", or "desire". It is considered one of the main obstacles to spiritual liberation, along with द्वेषः (hatred) and मोहः (delusion).
3. **In a broader sense**, रागः can also mean "color", "hue", or "dye". This usage is less common compared to the musical and philosophical meanings.
