**Mātrā** 
Meaning: "measure"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 8, 9, 10, 11)](mandukyopanisat.md)

## Meaning

A measure, quantity, or metrical mora; in the Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad, a constituent sound-measure or letter (A, U, M) of the sacred syllable AUM.

---

## Definition

*Mātrā* (मात्रा) is a Sanskrit nominal noun signifying a "measure," "measuring rule," "quantity," "extent," "metrical unit," or "mora"—the duration required to pronounce a short vowel. In general Sanskrit prosody (*chandas*) and phonetics (*śikṣā*), *mātrā* serves as the standard unit of temporal measure for vocalic length and rhythm.

Contextually within the *Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad* (Mantras 1, 8–12), *mātrā* specifically denotes a constituent phonetic letter or sound-measure of the sacred monosyllable *AUM* (ॐ). The text establishes a formal structural identity (*mātrāśca pādā pādāśca mātrāḥ*) between the three audible *mātrās* of *AUM* and the three quarters (*pāda*) of the Self (*Ātman*):

1. **A** (*akāra*): The first *mātrā*, corresponding to *Viśva* (or *Vaiśvānara*), the waking state (*jāgaritasthāna*), on account of all-pervasiveness (*āpti*) or being first (*ādimattva*).
2. **U** (*ukāra*): The second *mātrā*, corresponding to *Taijasa*, the dream state (*svapnasthāna*), on account of superiority (*utkarṣa*) or intermediateness (*ubhayatva*).
3. **M** (*makāra*): The third *mātrā*, corresponding to *Prājña*, the deep sleep state (*suṣuptasthāna*), on account of measuring (*miti*) or reabsorption/dissolution (*apīti*).

Beyond these three phonetic units, the Upaniṣad presents *Amātra* (literally "without *mātrā*" or measureless)—the soundless fourth state (*Turīya*). While the three *mātrās* correspond to the relative, conditioned aspects of reality, *Amātra* represents the unconditioned, non-dual Self (*advaita*), devoid of parts, sound, or empirical distinction. In Advaita Vedānta meditation (*upāsanā*), the aspirant progressively merges each *mātrā* into the next (*A* into *U*, *U* into *M*) before resolving *M* into the soundless silence of *Amātra*.

---

## Etymology

Grammatically, *mātrā* (मात्रा) is a feminine noun (*f.*) derived from the primary Sanskrit verbal root:

1. **Root**: *Mā* (मा, Class 2/3), meaning "to measure," "to mete out," "to mark off," "to limit," or "to fashion."
2. **Nominal Suffixation**: The word is formed by attaching the primary nominal suffix *-trā* (tra / trā), which designates an instrument, means, or standard of action, to the root *mā-* (*mā* + *trā* = *mātrā*, literally "that by which something is measured" or "an instrument of measurement").

Cognate nominal and participial formations derived from the same root *mā* in classical Sanskrit grammar include:
- *Mātṛ* (मातृ, "measurer" or "mother").
- *Māna* (मान, "measuring," "dimension," or "standard").
- *Miti* (मिति, "measurement" or "cognition").
- *Mita* (मित, past passive participle, "measured, limited, bounded").
- *Pratimā* (प्रतिमा, "likeness, image, figure").
- *Amātra* (अमात्र, negative compound, "devoid of measure or sound-letter").
