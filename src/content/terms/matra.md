**Mātrā** 
Meaning: "measure"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 8, 9, 10, 11)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: मात्रा 
Transliteration: Mātrā 
Meaning: "measure (of any kind), quantity, size, duration, number, degree &c."

**Definition from A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy (John Grimes)**
Sanskrit: मात्रा 
Transliteration: Mātrā 
Meaning: "mode; measure; prosodial instant"
Definition:
1. The Oṃkāra is composed of the three modes, AUM, and a fourth Amātrā, silence (Turīya). 
2. It is the length of time required for pronouncing a short vowel. 

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: मात्रा 
Transliteration: Mātrā 
Meaning: "the syllable "
 
**Translation by Swami Krishnananda**
Sanskrit: मात्रा 
Transliteration: Mātrā 
Commentary: "the syllable "
