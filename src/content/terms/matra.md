Meaning: "measure;" a measure, quantity, or metrical mora; in the *Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad*, a constituent sound-measure or letter (A, U, M) of the sacred syllable AUM.

See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 8, 9, 10, 11)](mandukyopanisat.md)

---

## Definition

*Mātrā* (Sanskrit: मात्रा) is a Sanskrit nominal noun signifying a "measure," "measuring rule," "quantity," "extent," "metrical unit," or "mora," which is the duration required to pronounce a short vowel. In general Sanskrit prosody (*chandas*) and phonetics (*śikṣā*), *mātrā* serves as the standard unit of temporal measure for vocalic length and rhythm.

Contextually within the *Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad* (verses 8–11), *mātrā* specifically denotes a constituent phonetic letter or sound-measure of the sacred monosyllable *AUM* (ॐ). The text establishes a formal structural identity (*mātrāśca pādā pādāśca mātrāḥ*) between the three audible *mātrās* of *AUM* and the three quarters (*pāda*) of the Self (*Ātman*):

1. **A** (*akāra*): The first *mātrā*, corresponding to *Viśva* (or *Vaiśvānara*), the waking state (*jāgarita-sthāna*), on account of all-pervasiveness (*āpti*) or being first (*ādimattva*).
2. **U** (*ukāra*): The second *mātrā*, corresponding to *Taijasa*, the dream state (*svapna-sthāna*), on account of superiority (*utkarṣa*) or intermediateness (*ubhayatva*).
3. **M** (*makāra*): The third *mātrā*, corresponding to *Prājña*, the deep sleep state (*suṣupta-sthāna*), on account of measuring (*miti*) or reabsorption/dissolution (*apīti*).

Beyond these three phonetic units, the *Upaniṣad* presents the soundless fourth state (*Turīya*) as *amātra* (literally "without *mātrā*" or measureless). While the three *mātrā* correspond to the relative, conditioned aspects of reality, *amātra* represents the unconditioned, non-dual Self (*advaita*), devoid of parts, sound, or empirical distinction. In Advaita Vedānta meditation (*upāsanā*), the aspirant progressively merges each *mātrā* into the next (*A* into *U*, *U* into *M*) before resolving *M* into the soundless silence (*amātra*).

## Etymology

Grammatically, *mātrā* is a feminine noun (*f.*) derived from the primary Sanskrit verbal root:

1. **Root**: *Mā* (मा, Class 2/3), meaning "to measure," "to mete out," "to mark off," "to limit," or "to fashion."
2. **Nominal suffixation**: The word is formed by attaching the primary nominal suffix *-trā* (tra / trā), which designates an instrument, means, or standard of action, to the root *mā-* (*mā* + *trā* = *mātrā*, literally "that by which something is measured" or "an instrument of measurement").

Cognate nominal and participial formations derived from the same root *mā* in classical Sanskrit grammar include:

- *Mātṛ* (मातृ) - "measurer" or "mother".
- *Māna* (मान) - "measuring," "dimension," or "standard".
- *Miti* (मिति) - "measurement" or "cognition".
- *Mita* (मित) - past passive participle, "measured, limited, bounded".
- *Pratimā* (प्रतिमा) - "likeness, image, figure".
- *Amātra* (अमात्र) - negative compound, "devoid of measure or sound-letter".
