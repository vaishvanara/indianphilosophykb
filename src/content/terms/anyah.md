**Anyaḥ**
Meaning: "other"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 1)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: अन्य
Transliteration: Anya 
Meaning: "other, different; other than, different from, opposed to (abl. or in comp.) another; another person; one of a number"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: अन्यः, अन्यत् 
Transliteration: Anyaḥ, Anyat 
Meaning: "the other"

**Translation by Chip Hartranft** 
Sanskrit: अन्यः, 
Transliteration: Anyaḥ
Meaning: "other"

**Translation by I. K. Taimni** 
Sanskrit: अन्यः, 
Transliteration: Anyaḥ
Meaning: "the other"
