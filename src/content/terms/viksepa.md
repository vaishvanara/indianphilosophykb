**Vikṣepa**
Meaning: "projection; false projection; distraction"
Definition: "It is the projecting power of ignorance, according to Advaita Vedānta. (See Avidyā.)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
