**Apītiḥ**
Meaning: "the support"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 11)

Reference:
Sharvananda, Swami (1920). Mundaka and Mandukya Upanishads: With Sanskrit Text; Paraphrase with Word-For-Word Literal Translation, English, Rendering and Comments. Mylapore, Madras: Sri Ramakrishna Math

-----------------------
अपीतिः (apītiḥ) is a nominative singular form of the Sanskrit word अपीति (apīti), which means "not a friend" or "not a companion".

In various contexts, अपीतिः can be translated to:

- Not a friend
- Not a companion
- Not a associate
- Not a ally
- Not a confidant

For example, in the Bhagavad Gita (6.32), it is written:

अपीतिः प्रजानां च

Apītiḥ prajānāṃ ca

Which translates to: "And one who is not a friend of the people."

In general, अपीतिः refers to a person who is not a friend or companion, and is often used to describe a lack of friendship or camaraderie.
