**Amitābha**
Meaning: “immeasurable glory”
Definition: The Buddha of unmeasurable splendor. He is the Buddha of the three forms: The Dharmakāya or Absolute, the Sambhogakāya or Divine Incarnation, and the Nirmāṇakāya or historical Buddha.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
