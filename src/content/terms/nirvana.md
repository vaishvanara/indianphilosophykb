**Nirvāṇa**
Meaning: “extinction; perfection; the Great Peace; "blowing out"; "cooling" (from the verb root vā = "to blow" + nir = "out")”
Definition: It is a state of peace and the Buddha said that it is unknown, unique, uncreated, and uncultured.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
