**Kuṇḍalinī** 
Meaning: “serpent; life force; a type of Yoga; coiled; winding; spiraled; coiled one”
Definition: The terms used to refer to the latent spiritual power envisioned in the form of a snake coiled three times about the center (Mūlādhāra Cakra) at the base of the spine.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
