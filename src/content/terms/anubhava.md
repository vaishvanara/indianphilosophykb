**Anubhava**
Meaning: "perception; direct presentation; knowledge; experience"
Definition:
1. According to Jainism, the result or retribution of an action; intensity.
2. According to Advaita Vedānta, it is the final court of appeal: hear the truth—Śruti; reflect upon it—Yukti; direct personal experience—Anubhava.
