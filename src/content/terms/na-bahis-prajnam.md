**Na Bahiṣ Prajñaṃ**
Meaning: "not consciousness outward"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 7)

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: न बहिष्प्रज्ञं
Transliteration: Na Bahiṣ Prajñaṃ
Meaning: "Nor outwards conscious"
Commentary: "i.e., the negation of Viśva or the awakened state."

**Translation by Swami Krishnananda**
Sanskrit: न बहिष्प्रज्ञं
Transliteration: Na Bahiṣ Prajñaṃ
Meaning: "not external consciousness like waking"
