**Catvāri Ārya-Satyāni** 
Meaning: “the four noble truths”
Definition: The central teaching of the Buddha. These truths are Duḥkha (suffering and pain), Duḥkha-Samudaya (the cause and origin of suffering), Duḥkha-Nirodha (the cessation of suffering), and Duḥkha-Nirodha-Mārga (the path to the cessation of suffering). 

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
