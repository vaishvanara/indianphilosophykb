**Sarvajña**
Meaning: "all-knowing"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 6)

सर्वज्ञ (Sarvajña) is a Sanskrit word that means "all-knowing" or "omniscient". It is an adjective that is often used to describe someone who has complete knowledge or understanding of everything.

In Sanskrit, the word सर्वज्ञ is often used to describe a being or entity that is considered to be all-knowing, all-seeing, and all-powerful. This being is often referred to as the ultimate reality or the supreme being.

For example, the phrase "सर्वज्ञ पुरुष" (Sarvajña puruṣa) means "the all-knowing person" or "the omniscient being", and is often used to describe a person who is considered to be all-knowing and all-powerful.

In a more literal sense, सर्वज्ञ can be translated to "all-knowing" or "omniscient", and is often used to describe someone who has complete knowledge or understanding of everything.
