कुले (kulē) is a locative singular form of the Sanskrit word कुल (kula), which means "family" or "clan".

In various contexts, कुले can be translated to:

- In the family
- In the clan
- Among the relatives
- In the lineage
- In the tribe

For example, in the Mahabharata (12.250.13), it is written:

कुले प्रजानां च

Kulē prajānāṃ ca

Which translates to: "And in the family of the people."

In general, कुले refers to the idea of being part of a family, clan, or lineage, and is often used to describe a person's social or familial relationships.
