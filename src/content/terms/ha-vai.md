**Ha Vai**
Meaning: "verily"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verses 9, 10, 11)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: ह
Transliteration: Ha
Meaning: "(prob. orig. identical with 2. gha, and used as a particle for emphasizing a preceding word, esp. if it begins a sentence closely connected with another; very frequent in the Brāhmaṇas and Sūtras, and often translatable by) indeed, assuredly, verily, of course, then &c. (often with other particles, e.g. with tv eva, u, sma, vai &c.)"
Sanskrit: वै
Transliteration: Vai 
Meaning: "a particle of emphasis and affirmation, generally placed after a word and laying stress on it (it is usually translatable by 'indeed,' 'truly,' 'certainly,' 'verily,' 'just'"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: ह वै
Transliteration: Ha Vai
Meaning: "verily"
