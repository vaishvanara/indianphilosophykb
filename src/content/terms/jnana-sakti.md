**Jñāna-Śakti**
Meaning: “the potency or power of knowledge”
Definition: The power of maintaining all objects in conscious relationship with oneself and with one another.
- ज्ञान – Jñāna – knowledge; wisdom; comprehension (from the verb root jñā – “to know”)
- शक्ति – Śakti –  power; capacity; energy; potency; Citi; Kuṇḍalinī; force; the divine cosmic energy which projects, maintains, and dissolves the universe; the spouse of Śiva (from śak = “to be able”)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
