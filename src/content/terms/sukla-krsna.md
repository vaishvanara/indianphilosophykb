# Śukla-Kṛṣṇā - शुक्लकृष्ण (White-Black / Mixed Karma)

Śukla-kṛṣṇā karma represents mixed actions that contain elements of both virtue (śukla) and vice (kṛṣṇā). For the vast majority of ordinary human beings, daily life is rarely purely saintly or completely malicious; instead, actions involve a complex mixture of motives, compromises, and dual outcomes. For instance, pursuing worldly success, business ventures, or even certain familial duties often requires competing with others or causing unintended minor harm while simultaneously doing charitable or well-intentioned deeds. Consequently, the resulting fruit is mixed—yielding a combination of pleasure and pain, success and failure, happiness and sorrow. This category applies to ordinary people caught in the complexities of worldly life, where complete purity is rare. Like pure white and black karma, mixed karma remains binding, continuously feeding the storehouse of latent impressions (karma-āśaya) and perpetuating the cycle of rebirth. Transcendence requires shifting away from mixed worldly motives toward entirely selfless action.
