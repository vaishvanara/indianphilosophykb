**Bhāti**
Meaning: "shining; self-luminous"
Definition: "In Vedānta, the Absolute (Brahman) is described as Asti (it is), Bhāti (it shines), Priyam (it is blissful). All physical entities partake eternally of these three while, momentarily, they partake of name and form (Nāma and Rūpa)."

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
