**Antaḥ**
Meaning: "internal"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 4)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Arthur Macdonell)**
Sanskrit: अन्तर
Transliteration: Anta-ra
Meaning: "a. intimate; inner; other; n. interior; interval, distance; entrance; time, while; opportunity, weak point; difference, species, peculiarity; surety"

**Definition from Glossary to the Record of Yoga (Sri Aurobindo)**
Transliteration: Antaḥ
Meaning: "within; same as Antar; short for Antardarśī or Antardṛṣṭa"

**Translation by Chip Hartranft**
Sanskrit: अन्तः
Transliteration: Antaḥ
Meaning: "inner"

**Translation by I.K. Taimni**
Sanskrit: अन्तः
Transliteration: Antaḥ
Meaning: "inner; internal"
