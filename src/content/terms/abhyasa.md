## Definition

Grammatically, *abhyāsa* (Sanskrit: अभ्यास; IAST: *abhyāsa*) is a masculine Sanskrit noun (*m.*). 

Lexically and philosophically, the term possesses ordinary, technical, and spiritual meanings across classical Sanskrit literature:

- **Literal and ordinary meaning**: Continuous endeavor, constant practice, exercise, exertion, study, repeated recitation, or habituated application.
- **Grammatical technical sense**: In Sanskrit grammar (*vyākaraṇa*), *abhyāsa* denotes reduplication, specifically referring to the reduplicated initial syllable or stem of a verbal root used in certain tense formations and frequentative or intensive verb forms.
- **Philosophical and soteriological meaning**: In Indian philosophy, *abhyāsa* is a central methodological concept:
	  - **Yoga system**: In Pātañjala Yoga, *abhyāsa* designates the unbroken, sustained effort and spiritual discipline directed toward quelling mental fluctuations (*citta-vṛtti-nirodha*) and attaining cognitive stillness. It operates as one of the two primary pillars of spiritual practice alongside dispassion (*vairāgya*).
	  - **Vedānta and exegesis**: In Vedantic discipline, *abhyāsa* represents the continuous reflection and meditative repetition of Upanishadic truths (such as hearing, reflecting, and meditating: *śravaṇa*, *manana*, *nididhyāsana*) to dismantle habitual ignorance (*avidyā*) and subtle subconscious traces (*vāsanās*). Hermeneutically, *abhyāsa* (repetition of a key concept or statement) is also classified as one of the six textual marks (*ṣaḍ-liṅga*) used to determine the true spiritual purport of scriptural passages.

---

## Etymology

The noun *abhyāsa* is an established primary nominal derivation (*ghañ-anta*) constructed from a prepositional prefix and a verbal root:

1. **Prefix**: *abhi-* (अभि), an indeclinable prefix indicating "towards", "upon", "facing", or "repeatedly".
2. **Primary root**: *as* (अस्), a primary verbal root meaning "to throw", "to cast", or "to project". When combined with the prefix (*abhi-√as*), the compound verbal base means "to throw towards", "to apply oneself to", "to practice", or "to repeat".
3. **Nominal Formation**: The nominalizing process transforms *abhi* + *as* into the masculine noun *abhyāsa* (*m.*), literally signifying "the act of repeatedly throwing or applying oneself toward a target or activity," giving rise to the meanings "exercise", "discipline", and "habitual practice".
