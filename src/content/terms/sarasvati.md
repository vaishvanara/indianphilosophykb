**Sarasvatī**
Meaning: “she of the stream; the name of Brahmā's consort who presides over speech, knowledge, and the arts; the goddess of wisdom (from saras = “flowing” and vatī = “having”)”

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
