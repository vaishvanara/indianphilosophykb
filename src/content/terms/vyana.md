Vyāna is the pervasive, circulating vital air that flows outward from the core to the entire periphery of the body. Unlike the other prāṇas which have localized seats, vyāna pervades every cell, tissue, and limb, governing the circulation of blood, lymph, nutrients, and nerve impulses. It is the coordinating force that keeps all parts of the organism integrated, connected, and communicating seamlessly. Energetically, vyāna manages muscular movement, joint flexibility, emotional expression, and the coordination of the nervous system. When vyāna functions optimally, there is vibrant systemic health, a strong immune response, and a feeling of bodily wholeness. A blockage or deficiency in vyāna results in poor circulation, numbness, muscular tension, or a sense of fragmentation and emotional disconnection. Yogic movement and stretching help stimulate and harmonize this pervasive current.
