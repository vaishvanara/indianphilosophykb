**Darśana**
Meaning: ““sight”; vision; to have auspicious sight of; to see a great or holy being, either human or divine; sensation; apprehension; intuition; a philosophical school”
Definition: Seeing or being in the presence of a revered person, sacred idol, or sacred place. It refers to both "seeing" and "being seen."

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
