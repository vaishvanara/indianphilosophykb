**Bhakti** 
Meaning: “loving devotion”
Definition: The path of devotion leading to union with God; the state of intense devotional love for God or guru. It is one of the paths to liberation.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
