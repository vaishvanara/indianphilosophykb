**Atītam**
Meaning: Gone beyond, transcended

According to the search results, the Sanskrit word अतीतम् (atītam) means "the past" or "that which has gone by".Some key points about the meaning of अतीतम्:

- It is the past participle of the verb अतिता (atitā), which means "to go beyond, surpass, transcend".
- The word अतीतम् literally translates to "that which has gone by" or "that which has passed".
- It refers to the past, in contrast to the present (वर्तमानम्) and the future (भविष्यत्).
- In philosophical and spiritual contexts, अतीतम् can also mean "that which has been transcended" or "that which is no longer relevant".
- The word is often used in phrases like अतीतं परित्यज्य (aṭītaṃ parityajya) meaning "abandoning the past" or "letting go of the past".
