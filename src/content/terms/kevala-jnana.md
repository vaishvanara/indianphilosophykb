**Kevala-Jñāna** 
Meaning: “direct knowledge; immediate perception; omniscience”
Definition: According to Jainism, it is omniscience. It is the height of wisom—pure, perfect, and absolute. It transcends all spatial and temporal categories. It manifests itself in the individual when all of the limiting obstructions have been removed. It is independent of the senses. It is uncontaminated by doubt, error, and delusion.

- केवल – Kevala – oneness; absolute; alone; perfect; uncompounded
- ज्ञान – Jñāna – knowledge; wisdom; comprehension (from the verb root jñā – “to know”)
