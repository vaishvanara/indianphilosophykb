**Mahāmāyā**
Meaning: “great illusion; divine Power operating as identified with the supreme Lord”
Definition: A name of Śakti, the Goddess.

- महा – Mahā – “great”; mighty; powerful; lofty; noble
- माया – Māyā – the principle of appearance; illusion; marvelous power of creation; magical power; mystery; God’s power (from the verb root mā – “to measure, to limit, give form”); “that which measures”

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
