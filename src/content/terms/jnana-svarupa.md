Jñāna-Svarūpa 
Meaning: “knowledge as essence; embodiment of knowledge”
Definition: According to Advaita Vedanta, one's true nature is Jñāna-Svarūpa.

- ज्ञान – Jñāna – knowledge; wisdom; comprehension (from the verb root jñā – “to know”)
- स्वरूप – Svarūpa – natural form; actual or essential nature; essence (from sva = “own, self” + rupa = “form, shape, figure”)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
