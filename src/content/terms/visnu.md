**Viṣṇu**
Meaning: “the supreme Lord; the all-pervading”
Definition: A name for the all-pervasive supreme Reality. One of the Hindu trinity of gods, representing God as the sustainer.
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
