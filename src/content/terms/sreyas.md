**Śreyas**
Meaning: “good; beneficial”
Definition: In the Upaniṣads, there is a distinction made between Preyas, what is pleasing or pleasurable, and Śreyas, what is good or truly beneficial. One is warned never to be lured away by the merely pleasing, for there is no end to the pursuit of pleasure. 
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
