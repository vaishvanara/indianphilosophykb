**Drishti-Srishti-Vada**
"he, she, it, that, this"
Meaning: "the theory that "peception is creation""
Definition: According to Advaita Vedānta, a theory that regards the entire world as the fabrication of the individual's intellect. Before the objects of experience are perceived, they are held to be non-existent. The world is created by an imaginer who imagines it.
