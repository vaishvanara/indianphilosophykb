**Prathamā**
Meaning: "the first"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 3, 9)](mandukyopanisat.md)

According to the search results, the Sanskrit word प्रथमः (prathamaḥ) means "first" or "foremost".Some key points about the meaning of प्रथमः:

- It is the masculine nominative singular form of the adjective प्रथम (prathama), meaning "first" or "primary".
- The word प्रथमः denotes something that is first in order, rank, or importance.
- It can refer to the first person, first place, first time, etc.
- In grammatical contexts, प्रथमः is used to indicate the nominative case.
- The word is derived from the Sanskrit root प्रथ (prath), meaning "to spread" or "to expand".
- प्रथमः is often used in phrases like प्रथमः पुरुषः (prathamaḥ puruṣaḥ) meaning "the first person".

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: प्रथमा
Transliteration: Prathamā 
Meaning: "foremost, first (in time or in a series or in rank; earliest, primary, original, prior, former; preceding, initial, chief, principal, most excellent"
