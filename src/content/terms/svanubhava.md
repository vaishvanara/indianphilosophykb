**Svānubhava**
Meaning: “Self-realization; self-experience”
 
- स्व – Sva – self
- अनुभव – Anubhava – perception; direct presentation; knowledge; experience

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
