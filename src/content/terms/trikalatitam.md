**Trikālātītaṃ**
Meaning: "the threefold division of time"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 1)](mandukyopanisat.md)

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: त्रिकालातीतं 
Transliteration: Trikālātītaṃ 
Meaning: "the transcendental, beyond time"

**Translation by Swami Krishnananda**
Sanskrit: त्रिकालातीतं 
Transliteration: Trikālātītaṃ 
Meaning: "the three states of the world of time"

**Translation by Swami Paramarthananda**
Sanskrit: त्रिकालातीतं 
Transliteration: Trikālātītaṃ 
Meaning: "the three periods of time"
