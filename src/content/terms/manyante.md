**Manyante**
Meaning: "consider"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 7)

मन्यन्ते (manyante) is a third person plural imperfective verb in Sanskrit, which means "they think" or "they consider".

In more context-dependent translations, it can also mean:

- They suppose
- They imagine
- They believe
- They think that
- They consider that

For example, in the Bhagavad Gita (2.14), it is written:

मन्यन्ते तं नाहं प्रपश्यामि

Manyante tam na aham prapashyami

Which translates to: "They think that I do not see him."

In general, मन्यन्ते is used to describe the thoughts or opinions of a group of people, often in a philosophical or introspective context.
