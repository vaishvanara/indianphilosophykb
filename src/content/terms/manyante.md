**Manyante**
Meaning: "consider"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 7)](mandukyopanisat.md)

## Meaning

They consider, deem, or regard; specifically in the Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad (Mantra 7), where sages regard the non-dual Self (Turīya) as the Fourth.

---

## Definition

*Manyante* (मन्यन्ते) is a Sanskrit verbal form meaning "they think," "they believe," "they deem," "they consider," "they regard," or "they hold of opinion." 

Lexically, the word denotes the active mental process of holding a conviction, forming a judgment, or assigning a specific designation or status to an entity.

Contextually within the *Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad* (Mantra 7), *manyante* occurs in the climactic statement defining the ultimate reality of the Self (*Ātman*):

> *nāntaḥprajñaṁ na bahiṣprajñaṁ nobhayataḥprajñaṁ na prajñānaghanaṁ na prajñaṁ nāprajñam | adṛṣṭam avyavahāryam agrāhyam alakṣaṇam acintyam avyapadeśyam ekātmapratyayasāraṁ prapañcopaśamaṁ śāntaṁ śivam advaitaṁ caturthaṁ manyante sa ātmā sa vijñeyaḥ ||* (Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad, 7)

In this passage, *manyante* ("they consider" or "they regard") functions as the key predicate linking the apophatic negations of empirical attributes to the positive designation of *Turīya* ("the Fourth"). The text clarifies that enlightened seers regard this non-dual, unconditioned Reality—devoid of internal consciousness (*antaḥprajña*), external consciousness (*bahiṣprajña*), or massed sentiency (*prajñānaghana*)—as the "Fourth" (*caturtham*) relative to the three relative states of waking (*Viśva*), dream (*Taijasa*), and deep sleep (*Prājña*). As Śaṅkarācārya explains in his commentary, *manyante* here conveys that *Turīya* is not an additional numerical state, but the very essence of the non-dual Self (*Ātman*) recognized upon the cessation of illusory phenomena (*prapañcopaśama*).

## Etymology

Grammatically, *manyante* (मन्यन्ते) is a finite verb form (*tiṅanta*) functioning as the 3rd person plural present indicative Ātmanepada (*laṭ, prathama-puruṣa bahuvacana*) of the Sanskrit verbal root:

1. **Root**: *Man* (मन्, class 4 Ātmanepada), meaning "to think, believe, imagine, deem, consider, regard, or perceive."
2. **Conjugational Formation**: As a verb belonging to the fourth conjugational class (*divādi-gaṇa*), the root *man-* takes the conjugational affix *-ya-* (*śyan* in Pāṇinian grammar), forming the present verbal stem *manya-* (*man* + *ya*).
3. **Personal Termination**: To the stem *manya-*, the 3rd person plural Ātmanepada primary ending *-nte* (present tense) is attached (*manya* + *ante* = *manyante*, "they think / consider").

Related nominal and participial derivatives from the same root *man* in classical Sanskrit literature include *mata* (past passive participle, "thought, considered, approved"), *mati* (feminine noun, "thought, intellect, opinion"), *manas* (neuter noun, "mind"), *saṃmata* ("agreed upon, respected"), and *mīmāṃsā* (desiderative noun, "investigation, reflection, inquiry").
