**Nidrā** 
Meaning: “sleep (from the verb root drā = “to sleep” + ni = “down, into”)”
Definition: According to the Yoga school, sleep is the modification of the mind (Citta) which is the substratum of the knowledge of absence of any thing. Due to a preponderance of inertia (Tamas) in its fluctuation (Vṛtti), there is no modification of waking or dreaming. However, the state is still a modificaion, for, upon waking, one has the consciousness that one has slept well.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
