**Avadhi**
Meaning: “supernatural cognition; transcendental knowledge; clairvoyance; limit”
Definition: According to Jainism, it refers to an individual's ability to perceive without the help of the sense organs and the mind, things which have shape and form. All living beings possess this capacity in varying degrees, with limitations appropriate to each, imposed due to material (Karma) veils.
