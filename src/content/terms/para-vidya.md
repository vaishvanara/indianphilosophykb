**Parā Vidyā**
Meaning: “the higher knowledge; wisdom”
Definition: The Upaniṣads sometimes make a distinction between the higher and lower truth. In the Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad, the former is the higher knowledge of the Absolute (Brahman) and the latter is the knowledge of empirical things. Generally, it is the supreme knowledge of the Ultimate or imperishable Reality. It is knowledge of the Self.
