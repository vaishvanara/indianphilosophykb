---
title: "Rūpa (रूप)"
devanagari: "रूप"
iast: "Rūpa"
aliases:
  - "रूप"
  - "Rupa"
---

Rūpa is the subtle element of vision, shape, color, and luminosity, evolving from the preceding tanmātras. It manifests in the material world as the gross element of fire or light (tejas) and is perceived through the sense organ of the eyes (cakṣuḥ). Rūpa brings the universe into visibility, allowing objects to be distinguished by their outward appearance, brilliance, and structure. Philosophically, rūpa governs illumination—both literal light that reveals physical forms and metaphorical light that represents clarity, perception, and knowledge. In Vedantic thought, the entire phenomenal world is often described as a play of name and form (nāma-rūpa), denoting its transient, illusory (māyā) nature. Meditating on the impermanence of rūpa helps the seeker look beyond superficial appearances to recognize the unchanging spiritual reality underlying all forms.
