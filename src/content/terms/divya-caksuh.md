**Divya Cakṣuḥ** 
Meaning: “divine eye; the heavenly eye; wisdom”

- दिव्य – Divya – “divine; celestial; divine nature (from the root div = “to shine”)
- चक्षु – Cakṣu – eye; visual sense

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
