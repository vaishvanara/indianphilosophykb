**Sanskrita**
Meaning: “coming together, combined cause, compounded thing, constructed, well or completely formed, perfected, polished, refined”

Variation
Devanagari: संस्कृतम्
Transliteration: Saṃskṛtam (IAST)
Definition: the Sanskrit language
