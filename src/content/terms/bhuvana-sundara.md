**Bhuvana-Sundara**
Meaning: “God as cosmic beauty” 

- भुवन – Bhuvana – the universe; the world
- सुन्दर – Sundara – beautiful, handsome, lovely, charming, agreeable; noble*

References:
1. Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
