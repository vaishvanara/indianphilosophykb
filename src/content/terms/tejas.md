Tejas (also known as Agni) is the third gross element, representing fire, heat, light, and transformation. It evolves from the subtle element of form and color (rūpa) and is perceived through the eyes. Tejas is the active force responsible for transformation, metabolizing substances, generating warmth, and illuminating the darkness. In the macrocosm, it is embodied by the sun, stars, and thermal energy that sustain life on Earth. In the human body, tejas governs digestion (agni), cellular metabolism, body temperature, visual perception, and the mental capacity for discernment and intellect. Philosophically, fire represents the inner spark of wisdom, spiritual transformation, and the burning away of ignorance (tamas). It converts heavy matter into subtle energy, serving as the bridge between gross physical existence and higher awareness.
