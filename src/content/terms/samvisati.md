**Saṃviśati**
Meaning: "enters"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 12)](mandukyopanisat.md)

संविशति (saṃviṣati) is a third person singular present tense form of the Sanskrit verb संविशति (saṃviṣati), which means "to enter" or "to join".

In various contexts, संविशति can be translated to:

- He/She/It enters
- He/She/It joins
- He/She/It unites
- He/She/It combines
- He/She/It merges

For example, in the Mahabharata (12.250.13), it is written:

संविशति प्रजानां च

Saṃviṣati prajānāṃ ca

Which translates to: "And he enters among the people."

In general, संविशति refers to the idea of entering into a relationship, joining a group, or uniting with others.
