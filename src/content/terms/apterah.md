**Apteraḥ**
Meaning: "on account of pervasiveness"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 9)

Reference:
Sharvananda, Swami (1920). Mundaka and Mandukya Upanishads: With Sanskrit Text; Paraphrase with Word-For-Word Literal Translation, English, Rendering and Comments. Mylapore, Madras: Sri Ramakrishna Math

------------------------------
अप्तेरः (aptēraḥ) is a nominative plural form of the Sanskrit word अप्त्र (aptra), which is a verb that means "to reach" or "to attain".

In various contexts, अप्तेरः can be translated to:

- They reach
- They attain
- They obtain
- They get
- They achieve

For example, in the Rigveda (1.164.45), it is written:

अप्तेरः स्वस्ति नो रथे

Aptēraḥ svasti no rathē

Which translates to: "May they reach us in a state of well-being."

In general, अप्तेरः refers to the idea of achieving or obtaining something, whether it's a goal, a state, or a condition.
