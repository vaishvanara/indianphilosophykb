**Cakravartin**
Meaning: “universal monarch”
Definition: The “wheel king” or “counter of the spokes”; thus, the person who has made the universe his/her own—i.e., a liberated being.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
