**Prakāśa** 
Meaning: “shining; luminous; effulgence; illumination; Pure Consciousnes”
Definition: One of the two aspects of Paramaśiva, the Ultimate Reality. It is the principle of self-revelation which illuminates everything; consciousness; the principle by which everything else is known.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
