**Ca** 
Meaning: "and"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 1, 8, 9, 10, 11)

1. the 20th letter of the alphabet, 1st of the 2nd (or palatal) class of consonants, having the sound of ch in church.
2. and, both, also, moreover, as well as

Reference:
Monier-Williams, Monier (1899). A Sanskrit-English Dictionary: Etymologically and Philologically Arranged with Special Reference to Cognate Indo-European languages. Oxford: Clarendon Press
