Udāna is the upward-moving vital air, located primarily in the throat, neck, and head region. It governs speech, expression, laughter, crying, swallowing (deglutition), and the physiological maintenance of the body against gravity (such as standing upright and maintaining posture). Udāna is also the energy of growth, enthusiasm, and higher mental faculties. Most profoundly, in yogic and Vedantic philosophy, udāna is the subtle force responsible for the separation of the astral body from the physical body at the time of death, as well as the rising of consciousness during deep meditation or profound sleep. When harmonized through yoga, udāna allows a practitioner to transcend physical heaviness, elevate thoughts toward the divine, and master higher states of spiritual liberation (mukti).
