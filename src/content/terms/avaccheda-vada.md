**Avaccheda-Vāda**
Meaning: "theory of limitation"
Definition: The individual is but an abridgement of the Absolute (Brahman), according to Advaita Vedānta. The body-mind complex seemingly limits the infinite Self (Ātman), just as a pot seemingly limits infinite space. When the adjunct is dispensed with, one realizes the non-difference between the two. This view is attributed to Vācaspati Miśra. (See Ābhasa-Vāda; Pratibimba-Vāda.)
