**Sarvasya**
Meaning: "of all"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 6)](mandukyopanisat.md)

सर्वस्य (Sarvasya) is a Sanskrit word that means "of all" or "of everything". It is a possessive adjective that is often used to describe something that belongs to or is related to everything.

In Sanskrit, the word सर्वस्य is often used to describe something that is universal or all-encompassing, such as a concept or principle that applies to everything.

For example, the phrase "सर्वस्य श्री" (Sarvasya śrī) means "the glory of all" or "the universal glory", and is often used to describe something that is considered to be of great importance or significance.

In a more literal sense, सर्वस्य can be translated to "of all" or "of everything", and is often used to describe something that belongs to or is related to everything.
