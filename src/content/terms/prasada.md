**Prasāda** 
Meaning: “grace (from the verb root sad = to sit + the prefix pra = to settle down, grace, favor); a gift from God; an offering made to God which is then distributed to devotees with His blessings; clarity; tranquility; serenity”
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
