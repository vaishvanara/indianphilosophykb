**Prārabdha Karma**
Meaning: "karma that has already been set in motion and whose fruits are being experienced in the present life"

Prārabdha karma is a Sanskrit concept in Hindu philosophy that refers to the portion of past karma that has begun to yield results in the present life.The key points about प्रारब्ध कर्म are:

- It is one of the three categories of karma, the other two being sañcita karma (accumulated karma) and āgāmi karma (karma of future actions).
- प्रारब्ध कर्म is the karma that has already been set in motion and whose fruits are being experienced in the present life. It is the karma that has begun to fructify.
- This portion of karma cannot be avoided or changed. It must be experienced to the full, whether pleasant or unpleasant.
- The other two types of karma - sañcita and āgāmi - can be modified through spiritual practices and good actions. But प्रारब्ध कर्म has to be exhausted through experience.
- Accepting and undergoing प्रारब्ध कर्म with equanimity, without aversion or attachment, is considered a spiritual practice in itself. It is seen as an opportunity for growth and purification.
- After exhausting प्रारब्ध कर्म, a person is freed from the cycle of birth and death, provided they have also eliminated sañcita and āgāmi karma through spiritual means.
