**Tattva-Jnana** 
Meaning: “Brahman realization; apprehension of the real; knowledge of the categories”
 
- तत्त्व – Tattva – category; truth; the essence of things; reality; principle; “that-ness” (from tat – the nominizing suffix + tvam – “ness”)
- ज्ञान – Jñāna – knowledge; wisdom; comprehension (from the verb root jñā – “to know”)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
