**Naiṣkarmya**
Meaning: "freedom from action (Karma) and its influence"
Definition: "According to Advaita Vedānta, disinterested and dedicated action, selfless service, which serves to purify the mind and thus serve as an auxiliary to liberation.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
