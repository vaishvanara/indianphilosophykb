**Iti**  
Meaning: "thus"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 1, 8)

**Definition from A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy (John Grimes)**
Sanskrit: इति 
Transliteration: Iti 
Meaning: "thus"
Definition: Iti is used at the end of a work to denote its conclusion.

**Translation by Swami Krishnananda**
Sanskrit: इति 
Transliteration: Iti 
Meaning: "this, thus"
