**Svapna-Sthāna**
Meaning: "the dream state"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 4, 10)](mandukyopanisat.md)

**Definition from A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy (John Grimes)**
Sanskrit: स्वप्न
Transliteration: Svapna 
Meaning: "dream; the dream state"
Definition:
1. It is to be immersed in one’s own self. The state of consciousness called dream is also known as Prajñā. 
2. See Avasthā. 

Sanskrit: स्थान
Transliteration: Sthāna 
Meaning: "position; proximity"
 
**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: स्वप्नस्थान 
Transliteration: Svapna-Sthāna
Meaning: "whose field is the dreaming state of life"
