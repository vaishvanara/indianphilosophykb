**Sattva**
Meaning: “pure; steady; goodness; illuminating; bouyant; joy; pleasure”
Definition: One of the three qualities (Guṇa). Its nature is of pleasure and it serves to illumine.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
