Meaning: “cognition or realization of non-duality”
 
- अभेद – Abheda – non-difference; non-duality
- दर्शन – Darśana – “sight”; vision; to have auspicious sight of; to see a great or holy being, either human or divine; sensation; apprehension; intuition; a philosophical school
