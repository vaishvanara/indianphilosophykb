Meaning: "cognition or realization of nonduality; perception of non-difference or identity."

---

## Definition

Grammatically, *abhedadarśana* (Sanskrit: अभेददर्शन; IAST: *abhedadarśana* or *abheda-darśana*) is a compound Sanskrit noun (neuter gender, *n.*) formed from *abheda* (*m.*) and *darśana* (*n.*). 

Lexically, the term possesses both a primary literal meaning and an extended philosophical meaning:

- **Literal meaning**: The act of seeing, observing, or perceiving (*darśana*) non-difference, non-division, or identity (*abheda*).
- **Extended or philosophical meaning**: The direct cognition, spiritual insight, or philosophical realization of nonduality (*abheda-darśana*). In classical Indian metaphysics and epistemology (particularly within *Advaita Vedanta* and related non-dualist schools), the term designates the intuitive perception or higher cognitive state wherein an aspirant realizes the essential identity and non-separateness between individual consciousness (*jīva*) and the Absolute Reality (*Brahman*), transcending the illusory perception of empirical diversity and distinction (*bheda*).

---

## Etymology

The term *abhedadarśana* is an established Sanskrit determinative compound (*tatpuruṣa*), structured from two distinct lexical elements:

1. **Abheda (अभेद)**: Formed by prefixing the negative particle *a-* ("non-", "un-", "without") to the noun *bheda* ("difference", "separation", "division"). The noun *bheda* is itself derived from the primary verbal root *bhid* ("to cleave", "to split", "to divide"). Hence, *abheda* denotes "non-separation", "cohesion", "absence of difference", or "identity".
2. **Darśana (दर्शन)**: Derived from the primary verbal root *dṛś* ("to see", "to behold", "to view") combined with the nominalizing suffix *-ana*. It signifies the act or faculty of "seeing", "beholding", "perception", "understanding", or "insight".

When joined, the compound structure explicitly denotes "the vision, perception, or direct realization (*darśana*) of non-difference (*abheda*)".
