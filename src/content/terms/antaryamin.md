**Antaryāmin**
Meaning: "inner guide"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 6)

**Definition from A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy (John Grimes)**
Sanskrit: अन्तर्यामिन् 
Transliteration: Antaryāmin 
Meaning: "indweller; inner guide; inner ruler; the spark of divinity within; the “witness” who dwells within every living being (from Antar – “within, inner” + Yāmin – “guide”)"
Definition:
1.	The immanent form of God (present in the heart). 
2.	The cosmic form of the Self as associated with illusion (Māyā).  

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: अन्तर्यामि, अन्तर्यामिन् 
Transliteration: Antaryāmi, Antaryāmin
Meaning: "the indweller and controller of all "
