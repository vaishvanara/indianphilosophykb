**Kāraṇa-Citta** 
Meaning: “the causal mind; the cosmic mind”
Definition: According to the Yoga school, the causal mind is all pervading like ether.

- कारण – Kāraṇa – “instrument”; cause; the efficient or instrumental cause (from the causative form of the verb root kṛ = “to make, to cause to make”)
- चित्त – Citta – consciousness; mind; thought; apperception (from the verb root cit = “to perceive, observe, know”)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
