**Ajāti**
Meaning: “non-origination; birthless”
Definition: A term employed by both Gauḍapāda and the Mādhyamika school of Buddhism to mean that nothing is born and nothing dies (though for different reasons). It says that the world and the individual are not there, have not been born, and will not die. Nothing is real from an ultimate standpoint.

अ – A – not*
जाति – Jāti – birth, production; re-birth*
