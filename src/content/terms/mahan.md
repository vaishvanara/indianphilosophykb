**Mahān** 
Meaning: “great; the Great One; supreme”
Definition: Name for Lord Viṣṇu or Lord Śiva. An ancient Vedic name for the Supreme. That One which is impossible to adequately praise. That alone which is truly Great. 

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
