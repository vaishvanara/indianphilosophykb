# Śukla - शुक्ल (White / Pure Karma)

Śukla karma refers to bright, pure, or virtuous actions (puṇya). These are deeds born from goodness, charity, scriptural study, religious rituals, benevolence, and moral discipline. Because they stem from sattva guṇa and positive intentions, they produce pleasant fruits, auspicious circumstances, and happiness for the doer, both in this life and future births. However, from the perspective of classical yoga, even though śukla karma is bright and desirable compared to negative actions, it is still binding. It keeps the soul tied to the wheel of saṃsāra because it generates positive latent impressions (saṃskāras) and a storehouse of merit that must eventually be experienced. It typically characterizes the lives of wise people, ascetics, and virtuous individuals living within religious or moral frameworks, but it falls short of ultimate liberation (kaivalya), which requires moving beyond all dualities of good and bad karma.
