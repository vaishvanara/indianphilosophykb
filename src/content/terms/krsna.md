# Kṛṣṇā - कृष्ण (Black / Dark Karma)

Kṛṣṇā karma denotes dark, impure, or vicious actions (pāpa). Rooted in the lower qualities of tamas and rajas, these deeds are driven by mental afflictions (kleśas) such as ignorance, anger, greed, hatred, and cruelty—ranging from minor harmful behaviors to severe crimes like violence and malice. Because they inflict suffering on others and violate moral order (dharma), they inevitably produce painful fruits, sorrow, and adverse karmic reactions for the doer. Kṛṣṇā karma generates heavy, toxic latent impressions that cloud the mind, lower consciousness, and lead to unfortunate births or miserable life circumstances. In yogic philosophy, avoiding black karma is the foundational step of ethical discipline achieved through the yamas and niyamas. While accumulating kṛṣṇā karma traps the individual in lower states of suffering and spiritual degradation, true liberation requires transcending both black and white karma altogether.
