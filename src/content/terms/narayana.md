**Nārāyaṇa** 
Meaning: “God in humanity; incarnate divinity”
Definition: The Godhead in humanity. Incarnate Divinity. The sole refuge of all creatures. The Universal Abode. One who has made the causal waters his abode. One who is the abode of all human beings. One who has made the hearts of human beings his abode. One who is the final goal of everyone.
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
