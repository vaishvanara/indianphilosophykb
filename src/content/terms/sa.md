**Sa**
Meaning: "that"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 2, 7, 8)

**Definition from Online Sanskrit Dictionary**
Sanskrit: स
Transliteration: Sa
Meaning: "he"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: स, सः 
Transliteration: Sa, Saḥ 
Meaning: "that, he"
