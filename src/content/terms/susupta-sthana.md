**Suṣupta-Sthāna**
Meaning: "the state of deep sleep"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 5, 11)](mandukyopanisat.md)

सुषुप्तस्थान (Suṣuptasthāna) is a Sanskrit phrase that can be broken down into three parts:

- सुषुप्त (Suṣupta) means "deep sleep" or "dreamless sleep".
- स्थान (Sthāna) means "place" or "position".

So, when combined, सुषुप्तस्थान (Suṣuptasthāna) can be translated to "the place of deep sleep" or "the state of dreamless sleep".

In yoga and spiritual contexts, सुषुप्तस्थान is often referred to as the fourth state of consciousness, beyond the three states of waking, dreaming, and deep sleep. It is said to be a state of profound relaxation, where the mind is completely still and the individual is in a state of unity with the universe.

In this state, the individual is said to be free from the limitations of the ego and the distractions of the senses, and is able to experience a deep sense of inner peace, clarity, and connection with the divine.

The concept of सुषुप्तस्थान is often associated with the teachings of the Upanishads and the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali, and is considered an important aspect of spiritual growth and self-realization.
