**Viśuddhi**
Meaning: “complete purification, purity, holiness, virtue; rectification, removal of error or doubt”
Definition: One of the seven wheels (Cakra). It is located in the throat center.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
