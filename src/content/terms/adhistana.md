**Adhiṣṭāna**
Meaning: "basis; substratum; ground"
Definition:
1. According to the Bhagavad Gītā, one of the five factors necessary for an action.
2. According to Advaita Vedanta, the Absolute (Brahman) is the only real Adhiṣṭāna.
