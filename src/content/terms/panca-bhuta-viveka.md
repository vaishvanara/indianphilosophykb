**Pañca-Bhūta-Viveka**
Meaning: "inquiry into the five elements"
Definition:
1. The title of the second chapter of the Pañcadaśī and a method which enquires into the nature of the five elements in order to demonstrate that the real is not the objective world made of the five elements.
2. According to Advaita Vedānta, when all of the elements are denied, only existence (Sat) remains. 
3. The principle employed is: What is grosser and more external is less real than the subtler, more internal, more pervasive. As the Self is the subtlest and innermost being, it is the most supremely real, according to Advaita Vedānta.
