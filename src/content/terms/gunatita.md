**Guṇātīta** 
Meaning: “beyond the qualities; transcending the qualities”
Definition: One who is free from, and gone beyond, the three qualities (Guṇas)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
