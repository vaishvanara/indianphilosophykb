**Utkarṣati**
Meaning: "increases"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 10)](mandukyopanisat.md)

उत्कर्षति (utkarṣati) is a third person singular present tense form of the Sanskrit verb उत्कर्ष (utkarṣa), which means "to excel" or "to surpass".

In various contexts, उत्कर्षति can be translated to:

- He/She/It excels
- He/She/It surpasses
- He/She/It outshines
- He/She/It is superior
- He/She/It is excellent

For example, in the Mahabharata (12.250.13), it is written:

उत्कर्षति प्रजानां च

Utkarṣati prajānāṃ ca

Which translates to: "And he excels among the people."

In general, उत्कर्षति refers to the idea of something or someone surpassing others in excellence, quality, or achievement.
