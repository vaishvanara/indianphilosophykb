**Dāna-Pāramitā**
Meaning: “benevolence; the virtue of giving”

- दान – Dāna – "giving"; gift; charity; alms giving; self-sacrifice; donation; generosity (from the verb root dā = “to give”)
- पारमिता – Pāramitā – highest ideals of spiritual perfection; virtues

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
