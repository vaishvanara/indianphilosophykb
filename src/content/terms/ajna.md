**Ājñā**
Meaning: “order, command, authority, unlimited power”

Sanskrit: आज्ञा चक्र
Transliteration: Ājñā Cakra (IAST)
Meaning: “the lotus of command; “command wheel””
Definition: One of the subtle centers of energy located along the spinal column. The spiritual center located between the eyebrows. The awakened Kundalini passes through this nerve plexus (Cakra) only by the command (Ājñā) of the guru, and for this reason it is also known as the Guru Cakra.
