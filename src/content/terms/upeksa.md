**Upekṣā**
Meaning: “indifference; equanimity resulting from disinterestedness”
 
Upekṣā is the supreme virtue of equanimity, mental balance, and unshakeable impartiality. As the fourth and culminating Brahmavihāra, upekṣā anchors and stabilizes the expansive emotional energy generated by loving-kindness, compassion, and sympathetic joy. It is the capacity to remain calm, centered, and clear-minded amidst the constant fluctuations and dualities of worldly existence—such as gain and loss, pleasure and pain, praise and blame, or success and failure. Crucially, upekṣā must not be mistaken for cold indifference, apathy, or callous detachment; rather, it is a profound, wise stability born from understanding the transient nature of phenomena and recognizing universal karmic accountability. It frees the mind from frantic grasping (rāga) and fierce aversion (dveṣa). By abiding in upekṣā, a practitioner achieves supreme emotional sovereignty, remaining peacefully anchored in their inner core like an unmovable mountain while the storms of life rage around them.
