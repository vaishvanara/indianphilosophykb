**Svādhyāya**
Meaning: “study; study of scripture; “self-study” (from sva = “self” + the verb root adhi-i = “to go over”)
Definition: One of the religious observances of Patañjali's Rāja-Yoga.
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
