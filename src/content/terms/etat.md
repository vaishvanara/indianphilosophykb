**Etat**
Meaning: "this"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 1, 2)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: एतत् 
Transliteration: Etat
Meaning: "(in comp. for etad)"
Sanskrit: एतद् 
Transliteration: Etad
Meaning: "(nom. m. of etad, q.v.) this, this here, here (especially as pointing to what is nearest to the speaker, e.g. eṣa bāṇaḥ, this arrow here in my hand; eṣa yāti panthāḥ, here passes the way; eṣa kālaḥ, here, i.e. now, is the time; etad, this here, i.e. this world here below)"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: एतत् 
Transliteration: Etat
Meaning: "this"
