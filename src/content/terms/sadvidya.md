**Sadvidyā**
Meaning: “meditation on the Absolute (Brahman) as the Real (Sat) without a second, as described in the Chāndogya Upaniṣad; knowledge of the Real”

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
