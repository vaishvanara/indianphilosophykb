**Bimba-Pratibimba-Vāda**
Meaning: "reflection theory; "theory of the original and its reflection""
Definition: The reflection theory of the Advaita Vedānta Vivaraṇa school, in which consciousness is said to be reflected in the adventitious condition, ignorance (Avidyā). According to this view, the individual is a reflection of intelligence present in egoity. As there exists no difference between a reflection and the original, the individual soul (Jīva) is non-different from the Absolute (Brahman).
