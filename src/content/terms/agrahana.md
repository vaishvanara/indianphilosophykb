**Agrahaṇa**
Meaning: "non-apprehension; not knowing the truth"
Definition:
1. According to Advaita Vedānta, it is an aspect of ignorance which remains even in the deep sleep state, though in a latent form. This explains how deep sleep differs from liberation. 
2. Non-apprehension is negative (Abhāva) and what is negative cannot be the cause of anything according to Advaita Vedānta.
