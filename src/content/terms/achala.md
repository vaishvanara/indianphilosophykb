**Achala**
Translation: “the immovable; standing still; firm; steady; unwavering; without change; mountain”
Definition: A state of total disregard for all pleasure or power.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
