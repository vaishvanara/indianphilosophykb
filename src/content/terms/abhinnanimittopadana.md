**Abhinnanimittopadāna**
Meaning: "non-different efficient and material (cause)"
Definition: "The Advaita Vedānta theory that the efficient and material causes are one and non-different: both causes are the Absolute (Brahman) since there is ultimately nothing but Brahman. Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta also accepts this theory.
