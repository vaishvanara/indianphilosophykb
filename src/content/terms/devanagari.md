**Devanāgarī**
Meaning: “divine city writing”
Definition: the Sanskrit script

- देव – Deva – heavenly, divine (also said of terrestrial things of high excellence)*
- नागरी – Nāgarī – of the character in which Sanskrit is usually written (probably from its having originated in some city)*

Sanskrit Letters in Devanagari Script**
1. अ — A — a — like a in organ or the u in but
2. आ — Ā — ā — like a in psalm
3. इ — I — i — like i in knit
4. ई — Ī — ī — like ee in meet
5. उ — U — u — like u in pull
6. ऊ — Ū — ū — like u in rule
7. ऋ — Ṛ — ṛ — like ri in Rit  
8. ॠ — Ṝ — ṝ — like ri in Rita
9. ऌ — Ḷ — ḷ — as in curl 
10. ए — E — e —  like e in they
11. ऐ — Ai — ai — like ai in aisle
12. ओ — O —  o — like o in go
13. औ — Au — au — like ow in how
14. अं — Aṃ — aṃ — Anusvāra (ṃ) is a nasal sound
15. अः — Aḥ — aḥ — Visarga (ḥ) is pronounced in the articulating position of the preceding vowel
16. क — K — k — as in kite
17. ख — Kh — kh — as in inkhorn
18. ग — G — g — as in give 
19. घ — Gh — gh — as in dighard
