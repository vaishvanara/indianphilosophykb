**Avākyārtha**
Meaning: "a sentence conveying a non-verbal sense; "not-spoken meaning""
Definition: "Any Great Saying (Mahāvākya); e.g., Tat Tvam Asi—That Thou Art, or Aham Brahmāsmi—I am Brahman, according to Advaita Vedānta."

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
