Tvam 
Meaning: "you" or "thou" 

The key points about the meaning of "त्वम्" are:

1. It is a personal pronoun in Sanskrit that indicates the second person, singular - "you" (singular).
2. "त्वम्" is one of the most fundamental pronouns in Sanskrit, along with "अहम्" (aham) meaning "I/me".
3. The term "त्वम्" is widely used in Hindu philosophical and spiritual texts, particularly in the context of the Advaita Vedanta concept of "Tat Tvam Asi" - "That Thou Art", which points to the non-dual identity of the individual self (जीवात्मा) and the ultimate reality (परमात्मा).
4. In this context, "त्वम्" represents the individual, finite self, which is ultimately revealed to be identical with the infinite, universal Self or Brahman.
5. The usage of "त्वम्" is crucial in the exploration of the true nature of the self and its relationship to the divine in various schools of Indian philosophy and spirituality.

So in summary, "त्वम्" is the Sanskrit pronoun meaning "you" or "thou", and it plays a central role in the philosophical and spiritual discourse around the nature of the self and its connection to the divine.
