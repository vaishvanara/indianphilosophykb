**Ātma-Jñāna**
Meaning: “knowledge of the Self”
  
- आत्म – Ātma – “breath” (from the verb root at = “to breath”) or (from the verb root āp = “to pervade, reach up to”); the inner Self
- ज्ञान – Jñāna – knowledge; wisdom; comprehension (from the verb root jñā – “to know”)
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
