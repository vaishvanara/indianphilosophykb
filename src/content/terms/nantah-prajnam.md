**Nāntaḥ-Prajñaṃ** 
Meaning: "not consciousness inward"
See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 7)](mandukyopanisat.md) 

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: नान्तः प्रज्ञं
Transliteration: Nāntaḥ-Prajñaṃ
Meaning: "Neither inwards conscious"
Commentary: "i.e., the negation of Taijas or the dreaming state."

**Translation by Swami Krishnananda**
Sanskrit: नान्तः प्रज्ञं
Transliteration: Nāntaḥ-Prajñaṃ
Meaning: "not internal consciousness like dream"
