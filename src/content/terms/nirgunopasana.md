**Nirguṇopāsana**
Meaning: “meditation on the attributeless Absolute (Brahman)”

- निर्गुण – Nirguṇa – attributeless; devoid of qualities; without attribute; the formless
- उपासन – Upāsana – meditation; worship; adoration; sitting near; spiritual discipline; attending

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
