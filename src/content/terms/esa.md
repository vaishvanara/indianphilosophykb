Meaning: "this, this here, he (pronominal); gliding or hastening (verbal adj.); seeking, search, wish, option (nominal)."

See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 6)](mandukyopanisat.md)

---

## Definition

In standard Sanskrit lexicography, eṣa (Sanskrit: एष) functions primarily as the nominative singular masculine form of the proximity demonstrative pronoun *etad* (एतत्), translating as "this", "this here", or "he". In syntactic usage, the pronoun indicates a person, object, or state that is immediately present or proximate to the speaker, distinguishing it from distal demonstratives such as *tat* or *adas* ("that"). It is frequently employed to refer back to a subject previously mentioned or to provide rhetorical emphasis when combined with personal pronouns. 

Secondary homonymous lexical entries designate *eṣa* as a verbal adjective or noun. As a participial adjective derived from motion, it means "gliding", "running", or "hastening". As a nominal derivative associated with desire or pursuit, it functions as an adjective meaning "seeking" or "wishing", and as a masculine noun meaning "search", "wish", or "option".

---

## Etymology

Pronominally, *eṣa* (एष) serves as the nominative singular masculine inflection of the demonstrative stem *etad* (or *eta*). The underlying pronominal base is constructed from the demonstrative element *e-* (derived from the pronominal root *a* or *i*) combined with the nominal suffix *ta* or *d*. Grammatically, the nominative singular suffix *-su* undergoes morphophonemic alteration (*sandhi*), transforming the sibilant *s* into the retroflex *ṣ* following the vowel *e*. The pronominal stem *etad* is cognate with Avestan *aêta*, Old Persian *aita*, and Armenian *aid*.

Beyond its pronominal function, *eṣa* exists as a primary nominal derivative (*kṛdanta*) from two distinct verbal roots:

1. From the verbal root *eṣ* (class 1 *bhūvādi*, *eṣati, -te*), meaning "to go, creep, glide, or hasten", yielding the participial adjective *eṣa* ("gliding" or "hastening").
2. From the verbal root *iṣ* (class 3/6 *divādi* / *tudādi*, *iṣati* / *icchati*), meaning "to seek or desire", forming *eṣa* as an agent adjective ("seeking") or an action noun ("search", "wish", "option").

The homonymous root derivations are philologically cognate with Avestan *aêsha* and Old High German *ērā*.
