**Sādhu**
Meaning: “holy person; saint; virtuous; good; sage (from the verb root sādh = “to go straight to the goal”); a monk or ascetic”
Definition: Any person who is practicing spiritual disciplines.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
