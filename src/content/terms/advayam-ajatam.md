**Advayam-Ajātam**
Meaning: “one non-coming-into-being; the birthless non-dual (Reality)”
  
- अद्वयम् – Advayam – non-duality, unity; identity (especially the identity of Brahma with the human soul or with the universe, or of spirit and mater); the ultimate truth.**
- अजातम् – Ajātam – unborn, not yet born, not yet developed**
