Vāk is the karmendriya of speech and vocal expression, operating through the vocal cords, tongue, and mouth. Philosophically, it represents the primary instrument of communication, through which thoughts, emotions, teachings, and sacred mantras (Vāk or Vāc) are externalized. In the Vedic tradition, Speech is revered as a divine, creative power (personified as Goddess Vāc), possessing the immense force to either heal or harm, bind or liberate. Vāk is deeply connected to the inner mind; disciplined speech based on truth (satya) and non-harming (ahiṃsā) is considered a foundational pillar of ethical growth. Yogic philosophy emphasizes mindfulness in speech, as unmonitored words dissipate mental energy and create heavy karmic bonds. Mastering Vāk eventually leads to vāk-siddhi (the perfection of speech, where spoken words manifest reality).
