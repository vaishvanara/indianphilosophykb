**Namaskāra** 
Meaning: “greeting (in a spirit of worship); "I bow to you"; the act of invoking "not-me" (but Thou); salutation; obeisance (from na = "not" + ma = "mine" + kāra = "to do")”

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
