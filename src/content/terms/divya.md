**Divyā** 
Meaning: “divine; celestial; divine nature (from the root div = “to shine”)”
Definition: A type of perception caused by the grace of the Lord. It is independent of the senses.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
