**Asat**
Meaning: "non-being; non-existence; false; the world of change; unmanifested"
Definition: "It is the basis of the universe, according to Advaita Vedānta. It is falsely real and really false. Sometimes it is said to be totally non-existent, as in the case of a square circle."

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
