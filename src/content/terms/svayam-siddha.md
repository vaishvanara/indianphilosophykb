**Svayaṃ-Siddha**
Meaning: “self-accomplished”
Definition: A type of perception independent of the senses and accomplished by yogic practices.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
