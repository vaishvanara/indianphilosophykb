**Bhavat** 
Meaning: "the present"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 1)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: भवत् 
Transliteration: Bhavat 
Meaning: "being, present"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: भवत् 
Transliteration: Bhavat 
Meaning: "the present"

**Translation by Swami Krishnananda**
Transliteration: Bhavat 
Meaning: "all that is now in the present"
