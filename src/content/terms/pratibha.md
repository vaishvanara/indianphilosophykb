**Pratibha** 
Meaning: “special mental power; imaginative insight; intuition; ever creative activity or Consciousness; the spontaneous supreme "I"-consciousness; Parāśakti”

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
