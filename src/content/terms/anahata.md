**Anāhata**
Meaning: ““unbeaten”; unstruck; continuous inner resonance; the heart; the heart center; the inner divine melody; Om”
Definition: The twelve-petaled lotus or subtle center of psychic energy lying in the central channel (Suṣumṇa Nādī). The lotus of the heart is the fourth plexus (Cakra).
