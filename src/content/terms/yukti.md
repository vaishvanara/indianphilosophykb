**Yukti**
Meaning: "reasoning"
Definition: According to Advaita Vedānta, the truth is realized through hearing the liberating word (Śruti), reasoning about it (Yukti) and finally, the final court of appeal, personal experience (Anubhava). 

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
