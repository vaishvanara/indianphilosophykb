**Cidākāśa**
Meaning: “consciousness space”
Definition: The subtle space of Consciousness in the thousand-petaled lotus (Sahasrāra) and/or in the heart.

Reference: 
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
