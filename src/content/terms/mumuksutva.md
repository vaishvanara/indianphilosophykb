**Mumukṣutva**
Meaning: “a burning desire for liberation”
Definition: It is one of the four qualifications for a spiritual aspirant. (See Sādhana-Catuṣṭaya.)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
