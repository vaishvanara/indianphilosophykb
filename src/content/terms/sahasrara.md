**Sahasrāra**
Meaning: “thousand; infinite; the thousand-petaled; the seventh subtle center”
Definition: The topmost spiritual center or thousand-petaled lotus located in the crown of the head. It is the seat of Śiva, the supreme Guru. When Kuṇḍalinī Śakti unites with Śiva in the Sahasrāra, the Yogi achieves the state of Self-realization.
