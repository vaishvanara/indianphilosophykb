**Ādinātha**
Meaning: “first or primal lord”
Definition: A name for Lord Śiva both as the Great God and as the first spiritual teacher (Guru) or perfect master (Siddha). 

Reference: 
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
