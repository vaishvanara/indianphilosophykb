Meaning: "first or primal lord; initial master; epithet of Śiva, Ādibuddha, and Ṛṣabha (the first Jina), and the primordial guru in the Nātha Sampradāya."

---

## Definition

*Ādinātha* (Sanskrit: आदिनाथ) is a masculine Sanskrit compound noun meaning "first lord," "primal lord," or "original master." In classical Sanskrit literature and Indian philosophical systems, the term serves as an epithet and proper name across several major traditions:

1. **Śaivism and Tantra:** The title designates Lord Śiva as the Supreme God, the supreme deity of destruction and transformation, and the primordial spiritual teacher (*guru*) or perfect master (*siddha*). In the Nātha Sampradāya (the tradition associated with Gorakhnātha and Haṭhayoga), *Ādinātha* specifically denotes Śiva as the supreme progenitor and head of the spiritual lineage of Nātha yogis.
2. **Jainism:** The term is a prominent title for Ṛṣabha (also known as Ṛṣabhadeva or Vṛṣabha), the first *Jina* ("conqueror") and *Tīrthaṅkara* ("ford-maker") of the present cosmological downward time cycle (*avasarpiṇī*), who established human civilizational order and taught the path to liberation.
3. **Buddhism:** In esoteric and Mahāyāna/Vajrayāna contexts, the term functions as an epithet for Ādibuddha, the "Primal Buddha" representing the primordial aspect of enlightened consciousness.
4. **Literary Usage:** The word also occurs as a personal name for classical Sanskrit authors, commentators, and figures in yoga treatises (such as Ādinātha Mahāyogin).

---

## Etymology

Grammatically, *Ādinātha* is a masculine noun (*m.*) formed as a dependent determinative compound (*tatpuruṣa*) from two primary nominal components:

* *Ādi* (आदि, m., "beginning," "first," or "primal"), derived from the prefix *ā-* (towards, taking in hand) combined with the verbal root *dā* (to give, take, or hold) and the nominal suffix *-i*.
* *Nātha* (नाथ, m., "lord," "master," "protector," or "patron"), derived from the Sanskrit verbal root *nāth* (to seek aid, protect, or rule).

The compound literal structure (*ādi* + *nātha*) signifies "the lord/master at the beginning" or "the primeval lord."
