Śrotra is the cognitive sense organ of hearing, responsible for capturing and processing sound waves and auditory stimuli. In cosmology, it originates from the sattvic aspect of the ego (ahaṅkāra) and is fundamentally connected to the subtle element of sound (śabda) and the macrocosmic element of ether (ākāśa). While the physical ear is merely the gross anatomical instrument, the true Śrotra is the subtle sensory faculty (indriya) that enables the mind to comprehend words, music, and environmental noises. Beyond ordinary communication, hearing is vital for receiving sacred scriptural wisdom (Śruti) from a teacher. In spiritual practice, turning the Śrotra inward allows a seeker to meditate on subtle inner sounds (nāda), guiding the mind toward deep concentration (dhāraṇā) and inner stillness.
