**Bhavati** 
Meaning: "becomes"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verses 9, 10, 11)

भवति (bhavati) is a third person singular present tense form of the Sanskrit verb भवति (bhavati), which means "to be" or "to exist".

In various contexts, भवति can be translated to:

- He/She/It is
- He/She/It exists
- He/She/It becomes
- He/She/It happens
- He/She/It occurs

For example, in the Rigveda (1.164.46), it is written:

भवति स्वस्ति नो रथे

Bhavati svasti no rathē

Which translates to: "He/She/It is our well-being in the chariot."

In general, भवति is used to describe the state or condition of something, whether it's a person, a thing, or an event.
