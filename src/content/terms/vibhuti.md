**Vibhūti**
Meaning: “sacred ash (Bhasma); manifestations of divine power; might; prosperity; welfare; splendor; exalted rank; greatness; superhuman power resembling that of God (Īśvara) (from the verb root bhū = “the existent, manifest” + vi = “apart”)”
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
