**Turīyatīta**
Meaning: the state of the individual soul in which it is in a totally blissful condition; beyond the fourth; the highest stage according to some Hindu schools
Definition:
1. The state beyond the “fourth” (_Turīya_). It is the supremely blissful state of complete freedom from all duality and the awareness of the one Self in all, the final attainment.
2. This is a term used for the individual in _Śaiva Siddhānta_.
