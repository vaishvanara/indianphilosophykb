**Mūlāvidyā**
Meaning: "primordial nescience"
Definition: "According to Advaita Vedānta, ignorance is the root cause of every appearance in the universe."

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
