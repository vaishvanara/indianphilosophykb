**Jyotiṣmatī** 
Meaning: “effulgent”
Definition: A state of mind which is pervaded by pure qualities (Sattva-Guṇa), resulting in tranquility.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
