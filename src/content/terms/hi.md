**Hi** 
Meaning: "verily"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 2, 5, 6)

**Definition from A Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Monier-Williams)**
Sanskrit: हि
Transliteration: Hi
Meaning: "(used as a particle and usually denoting) for, because, on account of (never standing first in a sentence, bu generally after the first word and used enclitically, sometimes after pronouns);  indeed, assuredly, surely, of course, certainly"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: हि
Transliteration: Hi
Meaning: "assuredly, verily"
