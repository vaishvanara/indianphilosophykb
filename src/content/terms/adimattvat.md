**Ādimattvāt**
Meaning: "on account of its being the first"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 9)

आदिमत्त्वात् (ādimatvāt) is a locative singular form of the Sanskrit word आदिमत्त्व (ādimatva), which is a noun that means "primacy" or "priority".

In various contexts, आदिमत्त्वात् can be translated to:

- From the beginning
- From the origin
- From the source
- From the first
- From the primary

For example, in the Bhagavad Gita (7.7), it is written:

आदिमत्त्वात् सृष्टं प्रलयान्तं च

Ādimatvāt sṛṣṭaṃ pralayāntaṃ ca

Which translates to: "From the beginning, and also from the end, it is created."

In general, आदिमत्त्वात् refers to the idea of something being derived from its origin, source, or beginning.
