title: "Pāṇi (पाणि)"

aliases:
  - "पाणि"

---

Pāṇi designates the hands as the active organs of grasping, holding, giving, taking, and physical craftsmanship. It is the primary instrument by which humans interact with, manipulate, and shape the material environment. Through the hands, internal intentions are translated into tangible actions, whether through daily labor, art, self-defense, or sacred ritual (such as forming mudras in yoga). Philosophically, Pāṇi symbolizes individual agency and productivity. Karmically, what one holds onto or lets go of reflects internal states of attachment or aversion. Yogic traditions teach that actions performed with the hands should be executed selflessly as an offering (sevā or karma yoga), detached from personal reward (phala), thereby transforming ordinary physical labor into a vehicle for inner purification and liberation.
