**Ātma-Sākṣātkāra**
Meaning: “direct sight of the Self; realization of the true nature of the Self; Self-realization”

- आत्म – Ātma – “breath” (from the verb root at = “to breath”) or (from the verb root āp = “to pervade, reach up to”); the inner Self
- साक्षात्कार – Sākṣātkāra – evident or intuitive perception, realization
