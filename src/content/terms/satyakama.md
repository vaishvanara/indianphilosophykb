**Satyakāma**
Meaning: “one who loves the good; the Being with eternal perfections; one whose desires are ever fulfilled”
Definition: Name of a truthful child in the Chāndogya Upaniṣad, who approached the sage, Gautama, and asked to be admitted to his school. Because he told the truth, he was admitted even though he did not know his lineage.
