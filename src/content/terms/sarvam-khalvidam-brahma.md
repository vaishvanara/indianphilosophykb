**Sarvaṃ Khalvidaṃ Brahma**
Meaning: “All this is Brahman”
Definition: A Great Saying (Mahāvākya) which occurs in Part 3, Chapter 14, Verse 1 of the Chāndogya Upaniṣad of the Sāma Veda.

- सर्वं – Sarva – all; complete
- खलु – Khalu  – no doubt
- इदम् – Idam – this, here, referring to something near the speaker
- ब्रह्म – Brahma – the one self-existent Spirit, the Absolute
