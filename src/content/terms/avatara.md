**Avatara**
Meaning: “divine descent; the descent of God into the world in a tangible form (from the verb root tṛī = “to cross” + preposition ava = “down”)”
Definition: It is sometimes translated as “divine incarnation” but it should be noted that the term refers to the supreme Lord appearing in this physical world in His/Her own Eternal Form out of His/Her own inconceivable prerogative. Noting this, the words <i>descent</i> or <i>incarnation</i> may be employed.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
