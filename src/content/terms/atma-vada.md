**Ātma-Vāda**
Meaning: "the theory of the Self"
Definition: "A belief in the abiding reality of the Self. Sometimes, the Advaita Vedāntins are labelled Ātma-Vādins."

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
