**Alāta-Śānti**
Meaning: “quenching of the fire brand”
Definition: Title of chapter 4 of the Māṇḍūkyakārikā in which Gauḍapāda explains the illusoriness of the phenomenal world by comparing it to the illusory designs produced by the waving of a fire brand.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
