**Bindu**
Meaning: “a dot, point; drop; crescent”
Definition: The compact mass of spiritual power or energy (Śakti) gathered into an undifferentiated point, ready to manifest as the universe. A form made of light (Blue Pearl). The material cause and substance of creation.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
