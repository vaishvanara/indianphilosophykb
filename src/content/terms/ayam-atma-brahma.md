**Ayam-Ātmā Brahma**
Meaning: “this Self is the Absolute”
Definition: A Great Saying (Mahāvākya) which occurs in the 2nd verse of the Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad of the Atharva Veda. 

- अयम् – Ayam – this
- आत्मा – Ātmā – the Self
- ब्रह्मा – Brahmā – the one self-existent Spirit, the Absolute
