**Bahiḥ**
Meaning: "external"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 3)

**Translation by Chip Hartranft**
Sanskrit: बहिः 
Transliteration: Bahiḥ 
Meaning: "external "

**Translation by I.K. Taimni**
Sanskrit: बहिः 
Transliteration: Bahiḥ 
Meaning: "outside; external world"
Commentary: "The word Bahiḥ is used because the Universal Mind is outside the individual mind and the world image in the individual mind has an external source."
