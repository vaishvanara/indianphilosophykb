**Na Bhavati** 
Meaning: "not born"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verses 10)

न भवति (na bhavati) is a negative form of the Sanskrit verb भवति (bhavati), which means "to be" or "to exist".

In various contexts, न भवति can be translated to:

- Is not
- Does not exist
- Does not become
- Does not happen
- Does not occur

For example, in the Bhagavad Gita (2.11), it is written:

न भवति संस्थितो ह्यहम्

Na bhavati saṃsthito hyaham

Which translates to: "I am not established."

In general, न भवति is used to negate the existence or state of something, and is often used to express a negative or contrasting idea.
