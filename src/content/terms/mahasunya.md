**Mahāśūnya**
Meaning: “the great emptiness or void”
Definition: The state of the formless Absolute, which is empty in the sense that it is without manifest creation. It is not a state of non-existence, because it has the nature of Being, Consciousness, and Bliss. 
 
- महा – Mahā – “great”; mighty; powerful; lofty; noble
- शून्य – Śūnya – the void; non-being; non-existence; negation; emptiness; zero (from the verb root śū, śvā, śvi = “to swell”)

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
