**Āpnoti**
Meaning: "attains"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 9)

आप्नोति (āpnōti) is a third person singular present tense form of the Sanskrit verb आप्नोति (āpnōti), which means "to obtain" or "to get".

In various contexts, आप्नोति can be translated to:

- He/she/it obtains
- He/she/it gets
- He/she/it acquires
- He/she/it receives
- He/she/it attains

For example, in the Rigveda (1.164.46), it is written:

आप्नोति स्वस्ति नो रथे

Āpnōti svasti no rathē

Which translates to: "He/She/It obtains us in a state of well-being."

In general, आप्नोति refers to the idea of acquiring or obtaining something, whether it's a possession, a state, or a condition.
