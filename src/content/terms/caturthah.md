**Caturthaḥ** 
Meaning: "the fourth"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 7, 12)

**Definition from A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy (John Grimes)**
Sanskrit: चतुर्थ
Transliteration: Caturtha
Meaning: "the fourth; the Turīya state; the transcendental Self beyond the three states."

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: चतुर्थं, चतुर्थः, 
Transliteration: Caturthaṃ, Caturthaḥ
Meaning: "the fourth"
