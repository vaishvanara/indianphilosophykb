**Yoga**
Meaning: “union; yoke”
Definition: A process or path or discipline leading to oneness with the Divine or with one's Self (from the verb root yuj = “to unite, join, connect”); the state of oneness with the Self, God; the practices leading to that state.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
