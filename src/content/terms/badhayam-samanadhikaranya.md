**Bādhāyāṃ Sāmānādhikaraṇya**
Meaning: "grammatical co-ordination in the sense of sublation"
Definition: "A grammatical theory used by the Advaita Vedāntins to interpret the Great Sayings (Mahāvākyā); e.g., originally, one perceived the object as a post, but later realized that it was a man. The original perception of the post is thus sublated."

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
