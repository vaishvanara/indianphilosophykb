**Manogupti**
Meaning: “equanimity of the mind”
Definition: According to Jainism, it is one of the external rules of conduct. It enables one to remove all false thoughts, to remain satisfied with oneself, and to hold all people to be the same.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
