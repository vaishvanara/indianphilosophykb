**Pratibhāna-Jñāna**
Meaning: “extrasensory perception; highest knowledge”
Definition: It is a type of perception directly perceived by the mind.
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
