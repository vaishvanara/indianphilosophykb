Meaning: "complete release, direct deliverance, or orientation towards spiritual liberation."

---

## Definition

Grammatically, *abhimukti* (Sanskrit: अभिमुक्ति; IAST: *abhimukti*) is a feminine Sanskrit abstract noun (*f.*). 

Lexically, the term encompasses both a literal action-oriented meaning and an extended philosophical sense:

- **Literal meaning**: The act of releasing, setting free, discharging, or letting loose towards or in the direction of a target or state.
- **Extended or philosophical meaning**: In Indian philosophy and soteriological literature (including *Vedānta*, *Sāṁkhya*, and allied schools), *mukti* denotes final emancipation, spiritual liberation, or release from karmic bondage and the cycle of transmigration (*saṃsāra*). Reinforced by the prefix *abhi-* ("towards", "intense", "facing"), *abhimukti* designates the direct, complete, or focused attainment of spiritual release, as well as the active orientation or movement of consciousness towards ultimate liberation.

---

## Etymology

The noun *abhimukti* is an established primary derivative (*kṛdanta*) formed by prefixing a prepositional adjunct to a nominalized verbal root:

1. **Prefix**: *abhi-* (अभि), an indeclinable prefix indicating "towards", "unto", "facing", "intense", or "complete".
2. **Primary root**: *muc* (मुच्), a verbal root of the sixth class (*cl. 6 P. Ā. muñcati, muñcate; passive mucyate*) signifying "to let go", "to set free", "to release", "to loosen", or "to discharge". Combined with the prefix, the complex verbal base *abhi-muc* (*abhi-√muc*) means "to let loose", "to release towards", or "to emit/discharge".
3. **Nominal suffix**: *-ti* (Sanskrit grammatical affix *ktin*), added to the primary verb root to form feminine abstract nouns denoting an action or state (*mukti*, "release, deliverance, emancipation").

The compound formation *abhi-* + *muc* + *-ti* yields *abhimukti*, literally "the state or act of being fully released towards or unto liberation."
