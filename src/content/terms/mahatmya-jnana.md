**Māhātmya-Jñāna**
Meaning: “knowledge of God's greatness”
Definition: According to Dvaita Vedānta, it is one of the steps leading to liberation. Here, the individual turns toward God and begins to study scriptures.
 
Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
