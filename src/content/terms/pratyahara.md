**Pratyāhāra**
Meaning: Abstraction

Pratyāhāra is the withdrawal of the senses from their external objects. It involves turning the attention inward, away from the distractions of the external world. Pratyāhāra helps to cultivate inner focus, concentration, and the ability to control one's senses and mind.
