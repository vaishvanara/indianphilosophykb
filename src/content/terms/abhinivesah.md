**Abhiniveśāḥ**
Meaning: "the persistent determination, intolerance, or prejudice of a bigot"

The key points are:
अभिनिवेशः refers to a strong, persistent attachment or determination, often in an intolerant or prejudiced manner.
It suggests an unwavering, biased perspective that is resistant to change or new information.

In the context of Indian philosophy, अभिनिवेशः is seen as an obstacle to spiritual growth and enlightenment, as it represents a clinging to limited, conditioned views.

The term conveys the idea of a rigid, dogmatic mindset that prevents one from achieving a more open, expansive understanding.

So in summary, अभिनिवेशः denotes an entrenched, prejudiced state of mind characterized by stubborn determination and an unwillingness to consider alternative perspectives.

Reference: Perplexity

---

Abhiniveśāḥ is the fifth and final kleśa. Patañjali notes that this intense self-preservation drive flows automatically by its own inherent force and is experienced even by the wise and learned. It is the primal, instinctual terror of annihilation, non-existence, and physical death. Because of avidyā (forgetting our immortal spiritual nature), the mind identifies completely with the physical body and instinctively views death as the ultimate tragedy rather than a natural transition. Abhiniveśā underlies many of our daily neuroses, anxieties, and desperate attempts to leave a legacy, accumulate security, or control the future. While necessary on a biological level for physical survival, spiritually it represents the ultimate bondage to the illusion of mortality. Overcoming abhiniveśā requires realizing the immortal, deathless nature of the true Puruṣa, which is neither born nor ever dies.
