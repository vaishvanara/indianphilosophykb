Meaning: "where, in which place, whither; when, on which occasion, if, in order that"

See: [Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 5)](mandukyopanisat.md)

---

## Definition

*Yatra* (Sanskrit: यत्र) functions primarily as a relative adverb denoting spatial location, meaning "where," "in which place," or "whither". 

In extended or non-spatial contexts, *yatra* carries temporal, conditional, modal, or conjunctional meanings. Temporally, it signifies "when" or "on which occasion". In situational or conditional expressions, it translates as "in which case" or "if". In Vedic or specialized clause-introducing usages, it functions conjunctionally to mean "in order that," "that" (such as following a question), or "in that" (*quod*).

The term frequently forms idiomatic adverbial combinations and compound expressions across classical and Vedic literature:

- **Reduplicated and indefinitized phrases**: *yatra yatra* ("wherever," "whithersoever"); *yatra tatra* ("in whatever," "anywhere whatever," "to any place whatever," "at every opportunity"); *yatra tatra api*, *yatra kutra*, and *yatra kutra api* ("anywhere soever," "everywhere"); *yatra kvā ca* (Vedic), *yatra kvacana*, *yatra kva vā*, and *yatra kva api* ("anywhere," "at any time," "hither or thither"); and *yatra vā* ("or elsewhere").
- **Compound formations**: *yatra-kāmāvasāya* ("the magical power of transferring oneself anywhere at will"); *yatratya* ("being or dwelling in which place"); *yatra-stha* ("dwelling in which place"); *yatra-sāyaṃ-gṛha* or *yatra-sāyaṃ-pratiśraya* ("having one's dwelling wherever evening falls"); and *yatra-astamita-śāyin* ("resting wherever sunset overtakes one").

---

## Etymology

*Yatra* is categorized grammatically as an indeclinable relative adverb (*avyaya*). Its derivation is well-established in traditional Sanskrit grammar and lexicography: it is formed by combining the relative pronominal stem *ya-* (or *yad*) with the adverbial locative suffix *-tra* (occurring in Vedic Sanskrit as *-trā*). Morphologically and syntactically, this construction serves as an adverbial substitute equivalent to the locative case (*asmin*) of the relative pronoun *ya-* across all genders and numbers.
