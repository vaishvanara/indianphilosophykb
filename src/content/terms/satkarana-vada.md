**Satkāraṇa-Vāda**
Meaning: "the theory that cause alone exists"
Definition:
1. Strictly speaking, the Advaita Vedānta theory of causation should be called this, instead of Satkārya-Vāda. The cause alone is real and ever existent, and all effects or phenomena in themselves are unreal.
2. See Vivarta-Vāda.
