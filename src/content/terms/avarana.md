**Āvaraṇa**
Meaning: "concealment; veil; screen; obstruction"
Definition: "The veiling power of ignorance. According to Advaita Vedānta, one of the twofold powers of ignorance (Avidyā)."

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
