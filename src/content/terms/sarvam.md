Sarvaṃ
Meaning: "all"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 1, 2, 9, 11)

**Definition from A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy (John Grimes)**
Sanskrit: सर्व
Transliteration: Sarva 
Meaning: "all; complete"

**Definition from Glossary to the Record of Yoga (Sri Aurobindo)**
Transliteration: Sarva 
Meaning: "all; the All"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: सर्व, सर्वं, सर्वान्, सर्वस्य
Transliteration: Sarva, Sarvaṃ, Sarvān, Sarvasya 
Meaning: "all"

According to the search results, the Sanskrit word सर्वं (sarvaṃ) means "all", "everything", "the whole", or "totality".Some key points about the meaning of सर्वं:

- It is derived from the Sanskrit root सृ (sṛ), meaning "to go" or "to move".
- The word सर्वं is the neuter nominative/accusative singular form of the pronoun सर्व (sarva), meaning "all", "every", "whole", etc.
- It represents the idea of completeness, totality, and universality. सर्वं encompasses everything without exception.
- In philosophical and spiritual contexts, सर्वं can refer to the ultimate reality, the absolute, or the divine essence that underlies all existence.
- It is often used in phrases like सर्वं खलु इदं ब्रह्म (sarvaṃ khalv idaṃ brahma), meaning "all this is verily Brahman".

So in summary, सर्वं is a Sanskrit word that conveys the meaning of "all", "everything", "the whole", or "totality". It represents the concept of completeness and universality in Hindu philosophy and spirituality.
