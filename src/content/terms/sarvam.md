Sarvaṃ
Meaning: "all"
From the [Mandukya Upanishad](mandukyopanisat.md) (Verse 1, 2, 9, 11)

**Definition from A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy (John Grimes)**
Sanskrit: सर्व
Transliteration: Sarva 
Meaning: "all; complete"

**Definition from Glossary to the Record of Yoga (Sri Aurobindo)**
Transliteration: Sarva 
Meaning: "all; the All"

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: सर्व, सर्वं, सर्वान्, सर्वस्य
Transliteration: Sarva, Sarvaṃ, Sarvān, Sarvasya 
Meaning: "all"
