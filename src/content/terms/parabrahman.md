**Parabrahman**
Meaning: "the supreme Being; the Divine as transcendent; that which is beyond all dualities (from para = "supreme" + brahman = "Absolute")"
Definition: "According to Advaita Vedānta, it is the supracosmic Divine who supports with its timeless and spaceless existence the entire cosmic manifestation of its own being in time and space. It is infinite, attributeless, and without name and form. (See Brahman.)
