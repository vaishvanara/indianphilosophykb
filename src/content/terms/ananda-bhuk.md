**Ānanda-Bhuk**
Meaning: "enjoyer of bliss (or peace)"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 5)

A Sanskrit phrase that conveys a sense of joy and delight!

आनन्दभुक् (Ānandabhuk) is a Sanskrit phrase that can be broken down into two parts:

- आनन्द (Ānanda) means "joy", "bliss", or "happiness".
- भुक् (Bhuk) is the root verb "भुज्" (Bhuj), which means "to enjoy" or "to relish".

So, when combined, आनन्दभुक् (Ānandabhuk) can be translated to "one who enjoys joy" or "one who relishes happiness".

In a spiritual context, आनन्दभुक् is often used to describe a state of being where one is fully immersed in the experience of joy and happiness, without any attachment or distraction. It is said to be a state of pure bliss, where one is fully satisfied and content.

In a more literal sense, आनन्दभुक् can be used to describe someone who takes great pleasure in enjoying good food, music, or other sensual delights. However, in a spiritual context, the phrase is often used to convey a deeper sense of inner joy and fulfillment that is not dependent on external circumstances.
