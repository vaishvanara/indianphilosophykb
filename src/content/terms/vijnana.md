**Vijñāna**
Meaning: “wisdom; cognition; intellect, consciousness; stream of consciousness (from the verb root jna = “to know” + vi = “apart”, hence, “to discern”)”
Definition: One of the twelve links in the causal chain of existence. One of the five aggregates. The intellect. It is also called Buddhi.

Reference:
Grimes, John (1996). A Concise Dictionary of Indian Philosophy: Sanskrit Terms Defined in English. Albany: State University of New York Press
