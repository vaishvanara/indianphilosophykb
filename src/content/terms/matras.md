**Mātrāś**
Meaning: "measure"

मात्राश् (mātrāsh) is a nominative plural form of the Sanskrit word मात्रा (mātrā), which means "measure" or "portion".

In various contexts, मात्राश् can be translated to:

- Measures
- Portions
- Quantities
- Amounts
- Degrees

For example, in the Mahabharata (12.250.13), it is written:

मात्राश् च प्रजानां च

Mātrāsh cha prajānāṃ cha

Which translates to: "And the measures of the people."

In general, मात्राश् refers to the idea of measuring or quantifying something, whether it's a physical quantity, a quality, or a characteristic.
