**Goptṛtva-Varaṇa** 
Meaning: “to seek God (Īśvara) alone as the protector”
 
- गोप्तृ – Goptṛ – one who preserves or protects or defends or cherishes*
- वरण – Varaṇa – the act of choosing, wishing, wooing, choice of a bride; honouring; the sacred texts recited at the choice of a priest*
