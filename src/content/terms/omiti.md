**Omiti**
Meaning: "Om" is this

ओमिति (Omiti) is a Sanskrit word that is often used to convey a sense of absence, non-existence, or negation. It is a term that is often used in philosophical and spiritual contexts to describe the state of being free from attachment, desire, or ego.

In Hinduism, ओमिति is often used to describe the state of liberation or moksha, where the individual ego and the attachment to the material world are transcended, and the individual experiences a sense of oneness with the universe.

In spiritual contexts, ओमिति is often used to convey a sense of detachment, non-attachment, or non-identification with the material world. It is a term that is often used to describe the state of being free from the cycle of birth and death, and the state of being one with the ultimate reality.

In a more literal sense, ओमिति can be translated to "not being" or "non-existence", and is often used to describe the state of being free from the limitations of the material world.

Overall, ओमिति is a powerful and sacred Sanskrit word that is often used in Hinduism and Indian culture to convey a sense of spiritual aspiration, detachment, and liberation.
