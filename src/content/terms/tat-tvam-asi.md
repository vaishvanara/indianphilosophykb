**Tat-Tvam-Asi** 
Meaning: “that thou art”
Definition: A Great Saying (Mahāvākya) which occurs in Part 6 (Chapters 8:7, 9:4, 10:3, 11:3, 12:3, 14:3, 15:3; 16:3) of the Chāndogya Upaniṣad of the Sāma Veda.

- तत् – Tat – that
- त्वम् – Tvam – you
- असि – Asi – are
