**Nāprajñam**
Meaning: "not unconsciousness"
From Mandukya Upanishad (Verse 7)

**Translation by Swami Sharvananda**
Sanskrit: नाप्रज्ञम् 
Transliteration: Nāprajñam
Meaning: "Nor unconsciousness"
Commentary: "i.e., the negation of all kinds of subconscious states."

**Translation by Swami Krishnananda**
Sanskrit: नाप्रज्ञम् 
Transliteration: Nāprajñam
Commentary: "It is not also absence of consciousness – Nāprajñam. It is not a state of inert perfection which the schools of thought like the Nyāya and the Vaiśeṣika describe."
